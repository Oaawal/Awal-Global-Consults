Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, email, Google profile and social pages are registered in your business name, and you receive every login at handover.

How long does it take?

A business email and Google Business Profile usually take a few days. A small website usually takes 1 to 2 weeks once we have your content. Google verification time depends on Google.

Do I need to be registered with CAC first?

No, but it helps. A registered name makes it easier to get a .com.ng domain and to verify your Google profile. We can handle your CAC registration too.

Can you update my website later?

Yes. Updates and ongoing support are available on request, quoted separately.

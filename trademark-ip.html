What is Intellectual Property?

Protect what makes your business unique

Intellectual property refers to the legal protection of creations such as brand names, logos, designs, written content, and other original work.

Without proper protection, your business identity or creative work can be copied, misused, or claimed by others.

Trademark registration and related IP support help ensure that what you build remains yours and is properly recognized.

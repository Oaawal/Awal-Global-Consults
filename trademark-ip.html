FAQ

Common questions about trademark registration

Is CAC registration the same as trademark registration?

No. CAC registration gives your business a legal identity, while trademark registration helps protect your brand name, logo, or identity.

Can I protect both my business name and logo?

Yes. Depending on your case, brand names, logos, and related identity elements may be eligible for protection.

Can I start the process remotely?

Yes. The process can begin remotely, and we guide you through the requirements clearly.

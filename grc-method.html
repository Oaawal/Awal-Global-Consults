Frameworks

The standards behind the work

International frameworks give structure. Nigerian law decides what is actually required.

FrameworkHow it is used
NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0Primary structure for gap assessments: six functions (Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) and 22 categories.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and Annex AControl reference for risk treatment and policies: 93 controls in four themes (organizational, people, physical, technological).
Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)Legal baseline for personal data: lawful basis, data subject rights, security, breach notification within 72 hours, cross-border transfers and NDPC registration.
ISO 31000 risk management principlesRisk method: identify, analyse (likelihood x impact), evaluate against appetite, treat, monitor and review.
Sector rules where they applyFor example CBN expectations for payments and fintech partners, SCUML/AML for designated businesses, and CAC record-keeping under CAMA 2020.
Method

Five steps, every engagement

1

Scope and context

Agree what is in scope, the legal and contractual obligations, and the target profile with management.

2

Current state

Interviews, walkthroughs of real settings and processes, and document review. Scores reflect evidence seen, not intentions.

3

Gap analysis

Each NIST CSF 2.0 category scored 0–5 now and at target. Gaps prioritised by the risks they leave open.

4

Risk assessment

Risks scored likelihood × impact, evaluated against appetite, treated with mapped controls, owners and dates.

5

Roadmap and review

A 90-day plan management signs off, then quarterly review of the register and progress.

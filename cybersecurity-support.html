The Real Risks

How Nigerian small businesses actually get hit

These are the incidents we see most. Each one is preventable with a few hours of set-up and a little training.

Hijacked WhatsApp Business

Someone tricks you into sharing a verification code, takes over your number and messages your customers asking for money.

"We've changed our bank account"

A fake email or message that looks like your supplier or client redirects a payment to a fraudster. The money is rarely recovered.

Lost data, then a regulator

A shared password or lost phone exposes customer data. Under the NDPA, a serious breach has to be reported to the NDPC within 72 hours.

Who This Is For

Built for businesses without an IT department

  • Shops, online sellers and service businesses that sell or take payments on WhatsApp and Instagram
  • Small offices where staff share computers, passwords or a single email account
  • Businesses that pay suppliers by bank transfer
  • Schools, clinics, NGOs and any business holding customer or patient data

What we don't do: we don't offer hacking or penetration testing, digital forensics, or recovering money from fraudsters. If you've just been hacked or defrauded, report it to your bank immediately, and we can help you secure your accounts and respond properly from there.

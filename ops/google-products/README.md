## Descriptions (copy into "Product description")

**Business Name Registration.** Register your business name with the Corporate Affairs Commission. We check name availability, prepare and file the registration, and deliver your CAC certificate. Government fees included, usually ready within 24 hours of complete details. Every quote is itemised before you pay.

**Limited Company Registration.** Incorporate a private limited company with CAC: name reservation, memorandum and articles, share capital and directors set up correctly, and filing. Government fees included for a standard company; usually 48 to 72 hours if CAC raises no queries.

**Incorporated Trustees Registration.** Register an NGO, church, mosque, association or foundation as incorporated trustees: name consent, newspaper publication, trustees' declarations and final approval. Government fees included.

**Company Limited by Guarantee.** Register a non-profit company limited by guarantee, including the Attorney-General's consent stage. Suits foundations, professional bodies and organisations with foreign involvement. Government fees included.

**SCUML Registration.** SCUML certificate for NGOs, real estate agents, car dealers, jewellers and other designated businesses, so your bank account isn't held up. Official registration is free; this is our professional fee to prepare and handle the application.

**Trademark Registration.** Trademark search, class selection, filing and follow-up with the Trademarks Registry. Itemised quote per class before you pay.

**CAC Annual Returns and Changes.** Annual returns for business names, companies and incorporated trustees, plus changes of directors, shareholders, address and share capital. Itemised quote.

**TIN and Tax Clearance Certificate.** Tax registration (TIN), filing support and tax clearance certificates for businesses and individuals. Itemised quote.

**Passport and NIN Support.** Help with Nigerian passport applications and renewals and NIN enrolment and corrections, including for Nigerians abroad. Itemised quote.

**Free 20-Minute Consultation.** A free call about CAC registration, compliance, passports or NIN. Book a time online in Lagos time; we call you on WhatsApp, phone or video.

**Legal and Advisory Consultation.** A 45-minute paid consultation on property documents, contracts, business structure and tax, foreign investment, expatriate quota and CERPAC, or student visas. From ₦20,000 depending on scope; the exact fee is confirmed before your session.

## 1. Free 20-minute check (the sales call)

Ask, in this order:
1. What does the business do, and how many staff and devices?
2. Which accounts matter most? (WhatsApp Business, email, Instagram/Facebook, online banking)
3. Who has the passwords? Do staff share logins?
4. How are suppliers paid, and who approves payments?
5. Do you hold customer data (names, phone numbers, IDs, health or school records)?
6. Has anything already happened? (hacked account, fake invoice, lost phone)
7. Has a bank, client or tender asked for security policies?

Close with the two or three biggest risks you heard and the matching services. Send an itemised quote
the same day.

## 2. Health check: what to review

- Two-step verification on email, WhatsApp, social media and banking
- Password storage and sharing; former staff access
- WhatsApp PIN, linked devices
- Payment process: bank-detail change checks, approvals, alerts
- Devices: screen locks, updates, lost-device plan
- Wi-Fi: default password changed, guest network
- Backups: automatic, tested
- Customer data: what, where, who can see it, privacy notice
- Staff awareness: phishing, code-sharing, reporting

Report format: summary (1 page, top 5 risks) → findings by area → fix-it list ranked
**Do this week / Do this month / Plan for later**.

## 3. Training session outline (60–90 minutes)

1. Why small businesses get targeted (5 min)
2. Phishing and fake messages, with local examples (15 min)
3. "Send me the code": WhatsApp and bank OTP scams (10 min)
4. Payment fraud and fake bank details (15 min)
5. Passwords and two-step verification, live demo (15 min)
6. Customer data and reporting mistakes (10 min)
7. Quick quiz and questions (10 min)

Leave the one-page reference sheet; issue attendance certificates; keep the attendance list
(useful as NDPA evidence for the client).

## 4. Breach response plan contents

Roles and contacts · how to contain (change passwords, revoke sessions, call the bank) ·
assess what data was affected · NDPC notification within 72 hours where required ·
telling affected people · record-keeping · lessons learned.

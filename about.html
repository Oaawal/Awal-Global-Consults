Our Story

Built for everyday Nigerian entrepreneurs

Awal Global Consults was founded with one clear purpose: to make legal and business processes accessible and understandable for everyone — not just large corporations with in-house legal teams.

We work with first-time entrepreneurs, small business owners, and individuals who need clear guidance and reliable support with registration, compliance, and documentation. Many of our clients come to us after a confusing or frustrating experience trying to navigate CAC or NIPO processes alone — our job is to make the next step obvious.

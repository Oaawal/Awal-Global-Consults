About these figures

Are these the official CAC fees?

The official charges shown are the CAC fees we pay on your behalf: name reservation, the filing fee and, for companies, stamp duty. Remita adds a small processing charge to each payment. Our own prices are shown separately as starting prices.

Why does a company cost more with higher share capital?

CAC charges a filing fee of ₦10,000 for each ₦1 million of share capital, and stamp duty is 0.75% of the share capital. So the official charges rise as share capital rises.

How long does registration take?

In our experience a business name is usually ready within 24 hours and a limited company within 48 to 72 hours if CAC raises no queries. These are our own turnaround times, not official CAC service times: CAC approval can take longer.

Is this a final quotation?

No. It is an estimate from starting prices. You get an itemised quotation within 1 working day of asking, before you pay anything.

Trust & Security

How we protect your information

Clients send us passports, identity numbers and company records. This page sets out the controls that protect them, the rules we follow under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and an honest assessment of where we still have work to do.

See our controls Privacy policy

In short:

  • Documents kept private, never sent as email attachments
  • One-time-code sign-in and a tamper-proof audit log
  • Clear retention periods, and erasure when they end
  • Breaches reported to the NDPC within 72 hours where required
Our controls

Organised by the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

Every control below is in place today and enforced by the system, not just written down.

Govern
  • Written privacy policy and termsPublished on this site and kept in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
  • Retention rules built into the systemApplications kept 6 years after closing (covering the 5-year AML minimum and CAMA/CITA record keeping); cancelled applications and enquiries 12 months. Every erasure is approved and logged.
  • Named responsibilityThe founder is accountable for security and data protection and reviews the daily system summary.
Identify
  • Known data and locationsApplication details and identity documents go to a private database and private document storage hosted on Cloudflare. We know what we hold and where.
  • Data collected only for the serviceApplication forms ask only for what the relevant registration or filing needs.
Protect
  • One-time-code sign-inStaff sign in to the dashboard with a 6-digit code emailed to an authorised address. Codes expire in 10 minutes, allow 5 attempts and are limited to 5 an hour.
  • Nothing usable storedSign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • Hardened sessionsSessions use a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie with the __Host- prefix and end after 7 days.
  • Private documentsClient documents are never public. Staff notifications link to the dashboard instead of attaching files to email.
  • Encrypted connections and security headersHTTPS everywhere with HSTS; anti-framing, MIME-sniffing and referrer headers; a strict Content Security Policy on the dashboard.
  • Bot protectionEvery form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, verified on the server.
  • Consent you can proveNewsletter sign-ups use double opt-in, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.
Detect
  • Append-only audit logEvery sign-in, status change, export and erasure is recorded. The database refuses any edit or deletion of the log.
  • Daily summaryAn automated daily check reports new activity, upcoming bookings and records due for review.
Respond
  • Breach responseSuspected incidents are contained first (revoke sessions, rotate keys), then assessed. Where personal data is at risk we notify the NDPC within 72 hours and tell the people affected.
  • Your rights handled on requestRequests to access, correct or erase your data are logged and answered; records the law requires us to keep are retained until their date.
Recover
  • Nightly backupsThe database is backed up every night to private storage and backups are kept for 30 days, so erased data leaves backups within that time.

Your rights

Ask what we hold, correct it, or have it erased

Under the NDPA you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, object to marketing, or ask us to erase it. Some records must be kept for a set period by law (for example AML and company records); we will tell you when they can be erased. See the privacy policy for details.

Report a security concern

If you think you have found a security weakness on this site, or received a suspicious message claiming to be from us, email info@awalglobal.com.ng with "Security concern" in the subject. We never ask for passwords or verification codes, and we only take payment into the company account named in our booking emails.

Who counts as a small company under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025?

A company with annual turnover of ₦100 million or less and total fixed assets of less than ₦250 million. It must meet both tests, and professional services businesses are excluded whatever their size.

Do small companies still file tax returns?

Yes. A 0% rate is not an exemption from filing. A nil return is required by law, and missing one costs ₦100,000 for the first month and ₦50,000 for each month after.

What do small companies not pay?

Company income tax (0%), capital gains tax and the 4% development levy. PAYE, withholding tax where it applies and CAC annual returns continue.

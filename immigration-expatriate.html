What are Expatriate & Immigration Services?

Support for international staffing and relocation processes

Expatriate and immigration services cover processes needed by foreign individuals working or residing in Nigeria, as well as businesses employing them.

These may include expatriate quota, residence permit support, business permits, and the regularization of foreign staff. Proper guidance helps reduce delays, confusion, and compliance issues.

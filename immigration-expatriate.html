What are Expatriate & Immigration Services?

Support for international staffing and relocation processes

Expatriate and immigration services involve processes required for foreign individuals working or residing in Nigeria, as well as businesses employing them.

These processes include expatriate quota approvals, residence permits such as CERPAC, business permits, and the regularization of foreign staff.

Proper handling of these processes is important to ensure compliance and avoid complications with regulatory authorities.

Registering a Nigerian Business from Canada

How Nigerians in Canada register a business name or company in Nigeria remotely: what to prepare, permanent residents and Canadian citizens, the ₦100m foreign-participation rule, signatures, and Canadian tax points.

Many Nigerians in Canada keep a business running back home: a family trade, an import-export line, property, or a new venture they plan to grow. You can register it from Canada without travelling: the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registration is online and every document can be sent from abroad.

If you plan to export from Nigeria, choose a company: the Nigerian Export Promotion Council does not issue export registration to business names. See export licence in Nigeria: NEPC registration explained.

Permanent resident or citizen: does it matter?

  • Permanent residents are still Nigerian citizens, so you register with your Nigerian passport or NIN like anyone else.
  • Canadian citizens who kept Nigerian citizenship can also register as Nigerians.
  • If you'll register a company using only a Canadian passport, the company has foreign participation and needs a minimum share capital of ₦100 million. These registrations are quoted individually, and further registrations such as NIPC may apply.

What to prepare

  • An international passport (or NIN for Nigerian citizens). A provincial driver's licence may not be accepted.
  • Your Canadian home address, for your personal details.
  • A business address in Nigeria. See do I need a Nigerian address?
  • A clear signature image and a passport photo.
  • Two or three business name options and a clear description of the business.

Send documents through our secure online application at awalglobal.com.ng/kyc, or by email or WhatsApp. You'll get a reference number to track progress at awalglobal.com.ng/track.

Signatures and notarised documents

A scanned specimen signature is normally enough for CAC registration. If a bank or another institution asks for notarised documents, they are usually notarised by a Canadian notary and then authenticated by the Nigerian High Commission or a Nigerian consulate in Canada. Ask the institution what it needs first. See signing CAC documents from abroad.

Canadian tax: a quick check

Canadian residents are generally taxed on worldwide income, and owning shares in a foreign company can bring Canada Revenue Agency reporting requirements. Take Canadian tax advice on your own situation before you register, especially if the business will be profitable or hold significant assets.

Can I register as a sole proprietor from Canada?

Yes: a business name is Nigeria's equivalent. It's quick and inexpensive, but the business and you are legally the same, so its debts are yours.

Who should be the director if I'm in Canada?

You can be the director yourself. Many founders abroad also appoint a trusted person in Nigeria, so someone can handle local matters day to day.

Will I need to travel to open a bank account?

It depends on the bank. Some complete onboarding remotely; others ask for a director in person or notarised documents. Ask before you plan travel.

The short version

From Canada, CAC registration is fully remote and usually done in one to three days. Register with your Nigerian passport if you can, choose a company if you'll export or grow, and check your Canadian tax reporting.

How Long Does Trademark Registration Take in Nigeria, and When Do You Renew?

Each stage of Nigerian trademark registration — search, acknowledgement, acceptance, publication and the final certificate — with realistic timelines, plus the 7-year first term and 14-year renewals.

BD Brand desk · Awal Global Consults 31 Oct 2026 · 3 min read
Reviewed by A.O. Abdullateef, Legal Professional, Founder of Awal Global Consults

On this page

  • The stages and how long each takes
  • Can I use my mark while I wait?
  • How long protection lasts
  • What slows registration down
  • The short version

Trademark registration in Nigeria is handled by the Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry. It's a multi-stage process. In our experience, the final certificate usually takes 12 to 18 months from filing. The good news is that once registered, your rights date back to the filing date.

The stages and how long each takes

StageWhat happensEstimated timeline
1. SearchCheck whether similar marks are already registered or pending — see trademark search1–3 working days
2. FilingApplication filed in the right class — see trademark classesYou get a filing date
3. Acknowledgement letterThe Registry acknowledges the application1–3 days after filing
4. Acceptance letterThe Registry examines the mark and accepts it. At this point your mark is partly considered approved2–8 weeks
5. PublicationThe accepted mark is published in the Trademarks Journal for 60 days, during which anyone can oppose itWaiting for publication can take a long time
6. Final certificateIf unopposed, the Registry issues the official certificate12–18 months from filing

These are our estimates from experience, not official Registry timelines.

Publication in the Trademarks Journal is the usual bottleneck. Timelines depend on the Registry, not the applicant — so file as early as possible, and keep your acceptance letter safe.

Can I use my mark while I wait?

Yes. You can use the mark, and you can show it as "TM". Don't use the ® symbol until the mark is registered. Your application is also a public record that someone searching will find.

How long protection lasts

7 yearsThe first registration term, counted from the filing date. After that, each renewal lasts 14 years.
  • You can renew in the three months before the registration expires.
  • Late renewal is possible within a limited window after the Registry gives notice of expiry, with a penalty fee.
  • If you don't renew, the mark can be removed from the register — and someone else could register it. See someone registered my name as a trademark.

What slows registration down

  • Objections at examination — the mark is too descriptive or similar to an existing one.
  • Oppositions after publication.
  • Wrong class or vague description of goods and services.
  • Missing documents, such as authorisation from the owner.

Can I speed it up?

There's no paid fast-track for the whole process. A clean search and a well-drafted application are the best way to avoid delays.

Does registration start from the certificate date or the filing date?

From the filing date. That's why filing early matters.

How much does it cost?

See trademark registration cost in Nigeria.

The short version

Search takes 1–3 working days, acceptance 2–8 weeks, and the final certificate usually 12–18 months, mainly because of publication delays. File early, because protection runs from the filing date. The first term is 7 years, then 14 years per renewal — diary the date.

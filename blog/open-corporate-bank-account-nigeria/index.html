Opening a Corporate Bank Account in Nigeria: What Banks Will Ask For

The documents Nigerian banks usually ask for to open a business or company account (CAC documents, IDs, BVN, references, board resolution and proof of address) plus the common reasons applications stall.

BD Business desk · Awal Global Consults 30 Oct 2026 · 3 min read
Reviewed by A.O. Abdullateef, Legal Professional, Founder of Awal Global Consults

On this page

  • Business name account
  • Limited company account
  • Incorporated trustees (NGOs, churches, associations)
  • Why account openings stall
  • Opening from abroad
  • The short version

Once your business is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), the next step is usually a business bank account. Every bank has its own forms and checklist, but they all ask for broadly the same things, because they must verify who owns and controls the business under anti-money-laundering rules.

Requirements differ between banks and change from time to time. Always ask your chosen bank for its current checklist before you visit, and ask whether any of it can be done online.

Business name account

Banks will usually ask for:

  • CAC certificate of registration and the registration details (status report or application form showing the proprietors).
  • Valid ID of each proprietor: international passport, driver's licence, voter's card or NIN slip.
  • BVN of each proprietor.
  • Passport photographs.
  • Proof of address: a recent utility bill for the business address.
  • References, often from existing account holders, on the bank's form.
  • Account opening forms and a mandate card with signatories.

Limited company account

Expect everything above, for each director and signatory, plus:

DocumentPurpose
Certificate of incorporationProves the company exists
Memorandum and articles of associationShows the company's objects and powers
Status report or particulars of directors and shareholdersShows who owns and controls the company
Board resolution to open the accountAuthorises the account and names the signatories
IDs and BVNs of directors and signatoriesKYC on the people behind the company
Details of shareholders with significant controlBeneficial-ownership checks
TINOften requested at opening or soon after. See TIN retrieval

Some banks also ask for certified true copies rather than ordinary copies. See CAC certified true copies.

Incorporated trustees (NGOs, churches, associations)

Banks will want the certificate of incorporation, the constitution, details and IDs of the trustees, a resolution of the trustees naming the signatories, and often a SCUML certificate. See SCUML registration.

Why account openings stall

  • Names that don't match across ID, BVN and CAC records.
  • Directors or signatories who aren't on the CAC record: file changes first; see adding or removing directors.
  • Missing board resolution, or one that doesn't name the signatories.
  • References not returned.
  • No proof of the business address.
  • Foreign directors without the residence or identity documents the bank requires.

Opening from abroad

Many banks require signatories to complete identity checks, sometimes in person, before an account is activated. If you're outside Nigeria, ask the bank about remote onboarding, and consider naming a trusted signatory in Nigeria. See choosing a local representative.

Can I use my personal account for the business?

You can for a business name, but a separate account makes records, tax and credibility far easier. For a company, it should have its own account in its exact registered name.

Do I need a BVN to be a signatory?

Nigerian banks generally require a BVN for every signatory. Ask your bank how it handles foreign signatories.

How long does it take?

It depends on the bank and how complete your documents are. Incomplete documents and missing references are the usual causes of delay.

The short version

Bring your CAC documents, every director's or proprietor's ID and BVN, proof of address, references and, for a company, a board resolution naming the signatories. Make sure names match everywhere and the CAC record is up to date.

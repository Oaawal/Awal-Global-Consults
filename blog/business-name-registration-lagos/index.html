Business Name Registration in Lagos: Cost, Documents and Timeline

Registering a business name in Lagos in 2026: what it costs, the documents you need, how long it takes, and what Lagos businesses need after registration.

BD Business desk · Awal Global Consults 2 Nov 2026 · 2 min read
Reviewed by Abdullateef Olamilekan Abdulwaheed, GRC Consultant, Founder of Awal Global Consults

On this page

  • What it costs
  • What you need
  • After registration in Lagos
  • Do I need to visit an office?
  • The short version

Business names are registered nationally by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), so the process is the same in Lagos as anywhere in Nigeria. What changes in Lagos is what comes after registration: state tax, local permits and the documents banks ask for.

What it costs

₦40,000Our business name registration package for Lagos businesses, government fees included. Usually ready within 24 hours of complete details.

The official CAC fees inside that are ₦1,000 for the name and ₦20,000 for registration, plus Remita charges. See business name registration cost and CAC fees 2026.

What you need

  • Two or three proposed names
  • A clear description of the business
  • The business address in Lagos, with the LGA
  • For each owner: full name, date of birth, address, phone, email, valid ID, passport photograph and signature

Full list: business name document checklist.

After registration in Lagos

StepWhy
TIN and taxThe owner pays personal income tax to the Lagos State Internal Revenue Service (LIRS) if they live in Lagos. See how business names are taxed
Bank accountBanks ask for your certificate, ID, BVN and a recent utility bill. See opening a business bank account
Local permitsSome local governments and Lagos State agencies require signage or premises permits. See which licences you need
Annual returnsBusiness names file every year by 30 June, except the year of registration. See CAC annual returns

Do I need to visit an office?

No. Registration is online, so you can register from anywhere in Lagos without travelling. If you'd like to talk it through first, our office is at 12 Hakeem Balogun Street, Agidingbi, Alausa, Ikeja, or you can book a free 20-minute call.

Is it cheaper to register in another state?

No. CAC fees are the same nationwide. Your business address is where the business actually operates.

Can I use my home address in Lagos?

Yes, if that's where the business operates. See using a Nigerian address.

Should I register a company instead?

If you want limited liability or plan to bring in partners or investors, consider a company. See business name vs limited company.

The short version

Registering a business name in Lagos is the same CAC process as anywhere: our package is ₦40,000 with government fees included, usually within 24 hours. Afterwards, sort your tax with LIRS, your bank account, any local permits and your yearly annual returns.

Changing Your Business Name, Address or Partners After CAC Registration

Which changes must be filed with CAC after registration (name, address, partners, directors, shareholders, share capital and business activity) how each is done for business names and companies, and why filing on time matters.

BD Business desk · Awal Global Consults 30 Oct 2026 · 3 min read
Reviewed by A.O. Abdullateef, Legal Professional, Founder of Awal Global Consults

On this page

  • Changes for business names
  • Changes for limited companies
  • Changing your company name: what changes afterwards
  • Moving from a business name to a company
  • The short version

Your CAC registration is a public record of who owns your business, who runs it, where it is and what it does. When any of those change, the record has to be updated, and the law expects it to be done promptly.

Keeping it current isn't just paperwork. Banks, regulators, tenders and partners check CAC records, and a mismatch between what's filed and reality is one of the quickest ways to have an account frozen, a contract delayed or a TCC refused.

Changes for business names

ChangeWhat's filed
New business addressNotice of change of address
Adding or removing a partner (proprietor)Change of proprietors, with the incoming partner's details and ID
Change in the nature of the businessChange of business activity
Changing the name itselfA consent application first; the name availability code it produces is then used to file the change of name on CAC's post-registration portal
Closing the businessNotice of cessation

Changes for limited companies

ChangeWhat's usually needed
Registered office addressBoard resolution, then notice to CAC
Appointing or removing a directorBoard or members' resolution, the new director's details and consent, then notice to CAC
Transfer of sharesShare transfer documents, updated register of members, then notice to CAC
Increase in share capitalMembers' resolution, then filing with CAC, and stamp duty on the increase
Change of company nameSpecial resolution, name availability check, CAC approval and a new certificate
Change of company secretaryResolution and notice to CAC

Company law sets short deadlines for most of these notices, and late filings attract penalties. File as soon as the change is decided, not when you next need a document from CAC.

Changing your company name: what changes afterwards

A company that changes its name keeps the same RC number and remains the same legal person: its contracts, assets and liabilities continue. But you'll need to update:

  • the bank account name,
  • your tax records,
  • licences and permits issued in the old name,
  • letterheads, invoices, website and signage.

Moving from a business name to a company

If the change you're planning is bigger (bringing in investors, limiting liability, or exporting) you may need a company rather than a change to your business name. See can I convert my business name to a limited company?

Can I change my business name's name without re-registering?

Yes. You apply for consent for the new name, and CAC issues a name availability code that is used to file the change of name through its post-registration portal. Companies change their name by special resolution and keep the same RC number.

Do I have to file a change before my annual return?

Yes. Changes are filed when they happen. Your annual return then confirms the current position. See CAC annual returns.

Can a director abroad sign the resolutions?

Yes. Directors abroad can sign and send documents remotely. See signing documents from abroad.

The short version

When your address, owners, directors, shares, activity or name change, file it with CAC promptly. Business names update proprietors, address and activity; companies need the right resolution first, then the CAC filing.

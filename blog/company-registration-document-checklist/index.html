Company Registration: Document Checklist

A complete checklist for registering a private limited company with CAC: proposed names, objects, address, share capital and shareholding, and each director's and shareholder's details, ID, photograph and signature, plus extra items for foreign shareholders and corporate shareholders.

Registering a private company limited by shares means making a few decisions as well as gathering documents. Use this checklist before you start.

Decide the share capital and who owns what before you file. These are the hardest things to change afterwards. See how much share capital to register.

Decisions to make first

  • Two or three proposed names, ending in "Limited" or "Ltd". See why CAC names get rejected
  • Business objects: a clear description of what the company will do
  • Registered office address in Nigeria, with state and LGA. See do I need a Nigerian address?
  • Share capital: at least ₦100,000, or ₦100 million with any foreign shareholder. See CAC fees 2026 for how it affects government fees
  • Shareholding: how many shares each shareholder holds
  • Directors: at least one for a small company, two for others. See single-member companies
  • Company secretary, if the company isn't a small company
  • Persons with significant control: worked out from the shareholders and their shareholding, so get the shareholding right first
  • Company email and phone number

For each director and shareholder

  • Full name, exactly as on ID
  • Date of birth
  • Gender and nationality
  • Occupation
  • Residential and service address
  • Phone number and email address
  • Valid ID: international passport, NIN, Nigerian driver's licence or voter's card
  • Recent passport photograph (clear digital copy)
  • Signature: on plain white paper, clearly scanned or photographed
  • Number of shares (for shareholders)

Extra items in some cases

SituationWhat you'll also need
Any foreign shareholderTheir passport, at least ₦100m share capital, and later NIPC registration and other foreign-investment steps. See can a foreigner own a Nigerian company?
A company as shareholderIts certificate of incorporation, details of its directors, and a resolution appointing its representative
Directors or shareholders abroadInternational passport and remote signing. See signing CAC documents from abroad
Regulated businessCheck sector licences and any capital requirements first. See which licences you need
Converting a business nameYour business name details. See converting a business name to a company

Before you submit: quick check

  • Names, dates and ID numbers match on every document
  • Shareholdings add up to the total share capital
  • Every director and shareholder has ID, photograph and signature ready
  • The objects cover everything the company will do
  • Partners have agreed what happens if someone leaves: consider a shareholders' agreement

For tax registration and your bank account

  • Recent utility bill for the business address: needed as proof of address for tax registration with the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) and when opening your bank account

After registration

Keep safe: the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, status report, and payment receipts. You'll need them for your bank account, TIN, licences and annual returns. See opening a corporate bank account and CAC annual returns.

Can one person be both director and shareholder?

Yes. For a small company, one person can be the only director and only shareholder.

Do I need a lawyer?

Not by law. See do I need a lawyer to register a company?

What does it cost?

Our packages start from ₦75,000, government fees included. Higher share capital means higher government fees. See CAC fees 2026.

The short version

Decide your name, objects, address, share capital, shareholding and directors first. Then gather each director's and shareholder's details, ID, photograph and signature: all matching exactly. Foreign or corporate shareholders need extra documents.

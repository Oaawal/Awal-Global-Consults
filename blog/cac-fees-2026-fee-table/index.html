CAC Fees 2026: Official Government Fees for Business Names and Companies

The official CAC government fees for 2026 — name reservation, business name registration, company registration per ₦1 million of share capital and 0.75% stamp duty — with worked examples for common share capital amounts.

RD Research desk · Awal Global Consults 31 Oct 2026 · 3 min read
Reviewed by A.O. Abdullateef, Legal Professional, Founder of Awal Global Consults

On this page

  • Business name fees
  • Company (limited by shares) fees
  • Worked examples for companies
  • What these fees don't cover
  • Fees can change
  • The short version

These are the official government fees paid to the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), as charged on the CAC portal in 2026. They are paid through Remita, which adds its own processing charge to each payment.

These are government fees only — not our package prices. Our packages (business name from ₦40,000, company from ₦75,000) already include the government fees for a standard registration. See CAC registration cost for everything that goes into a total price.

Business name fees

ItemOfficial fee
Name reservation₦1,000 + Remita charge
Registration (submission)₦20,000 + Remita charge
Total government fees₦21,000 + Remita charges

Company (limited by shares) fees

ItemOfficial fee
Name reservation₦1,000 + Remita charge
Registration (submission)₦10,000 for every ₦1 million of share capital + Remita charge
Stamp duty on share capital0.75% of share capital — ₦7,500 for every ₦1 million — + Remita charge

What these fees don't cover

  • Remita processing charges on each payment.
  • Professional fees for preparing documents, filing and handling queries.
  • Post-registration filings — annual returns, changes of directors or address, increases in share capital. See CAC annual returns.
  • Other registrations — TIN, SCUML, licences. See which licences you need.

Fees can change

CAC fees are set by government and change from time to time. This table reflects the fees charged on the CAC portal as of 31 October 2026. We update it when CAC changes its fees.

How do I pay CAC fees?

Fees are generated on the CAC portal and paid through Remita. Keep the payment receipts with your registration records.

Is stamp duty paid to CAC?

Stamp duty on share capital is paid as part of the company registration process on the portal, alongside the CAC fees.

Why is your package price different from these fees?

Our packages include these government fees plus our professional work: name search, documents, filing and following through to approval. See business name registration cost.

The short version

Business name: ₦1,000 reservation + ₦20,000 registration. Company: ₦1,000 reservation + ₦10,000 per ₦1 million share capital + 0.75% stamp duty. Remita adds a charge to each payment. Our packages include these fees, itemised before you pay.

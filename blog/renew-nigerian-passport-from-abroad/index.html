Renewing a Nigerian Passport from Abroad (UK, US, Canada)

How Nigerians in the UK, US, Canada and elsewhere renew their passport: the online application, payment, the biometrics appointment at the embassy or high commission, the NIN requirement, contactless renewal, and how to avoid delays.

DD Diaspora desk · Awal Global Consults 30 Oct 2026 · 3 min read
Reviewed by A.O. Abdullateef, Legal Professional, Founder of Awal Global Consults

On this page

  • The process, step by step
  • What to take to the appointment
  • Contactless renewal
  • Why renewals get delayed
  • The short version

You can renew your Nigerian passport without travelling home. The application and payment are done online through the Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS), and for most applicants the process ends with a biometrics appointment in person at the Nigerian embassy, high commission or consulate where you live.

You need a NIN. A National Identification Number is required for every passport application and renewal. If you don't have one yet, get it first. See how Nigerians abroad can get a NIN.

The process, step by step

  1. Check your NIN and make sure the details on it (name, date of birth) match your current passport. Mismatches are a leading cause of delays.
  2. Apply online on the NIS passport portal, choosing your country and the mission where you'll attend.
  3. Pay the fee online. Fees depend on the passport type (number of pages and validity). Pay only through the official portal.
  4. Book your appointment at the embassy, high commission or consulate.
  5. Attend in person for biometrics, photograph and fingerprints, with your old passport, payment confirmation and supporting documents.
  6. Collect your passport, or receive it by the delivery arrangement your mission offers.

What to take to the appointment

  • Your current or expired Nigerian passport (and a copy of the data page).
  • Your NIN slip or NIN details.
  • Payment receipt and acknowledgement from the online application.
  • Proof of legal residence in the country, if your mission asks for it.
  • For a change of name (for example after marriage), the supporting documents: marriage certificate, deed poll or similar.
  • For a lost passport, a police report and any other documents your mission requires.

Contactless renewal

NIS has introduced contactless renewal for some eligible applicants abroad, using biometrics already on file. Eligibility and availability depend on your country and your existing records. If you aren't eligible, the portal directs you to book a physical appointment instead, which is still how most people renew.

Why renewals get delayed

  • NIN details don't match the passport record.
  • Applying late. Appointments at busy missions can be booked up weeks ahead, so start well before your passport expires, especially if you'll need it for a visa or residence permit renewal.
  • Payment issues: paying through unofficial agents or links.
  • Missing documents at the appointment, meaning you have to rebook.
  • Backlogs at particular missions. NIS sometimes sends special intervention teams abroad to clear them, so watch your mission's announcements.
Treat your passport expiry date like a visa deadline. Start the renewal at least three to six months before you need it.

Can I renew without visiting the embassy?

Only if you're eligible for contactless renewal. Most applicants still attend in person for biometrics.

Can someone else attend the biometrics appointment for me?

No. Biometrics are your photograph and fingerprints, so you must attend yourself.

What can Awal Global Consults do if I still have to attend?

Everything around the appointment: checking your NIN and documents, completing the application, guiding the payment, booking the appointment and following up afterwards, so the one visit is all you need.

The short version

Get your NIN in order, apply and pay on the official NIS portal, book early, and attend for biometrics with the right documents. Check whether you qualify for contactless renewal, but plan for an in-person appointment.

Export Licence in Nigeria: NEPC Registration Explained

What the NEPC exporter's certificate is, why only companies can get it, the documents and steps on the NEPC e-registration portal, validity and renewal, and what else you need to export.

If you want to export goods from Nigeria — agricultural produce, processed foods, crafts, manufactured goods — the starting point is registration with the Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC). The exporter's certificate it issues is what people usually mean by an "export licence".

Why you need it

The NEPC certificate identifies you as a registered exporter. In practice it is what banks and other agencies look for when you start formal export transactions, including the documentation used to process export proceeds. Without it, exporting through official channels is difficult.

Only registered companies qualify. NEPC does not issue exporter registration to business names. If you're trading as a business name, the first step is registering a limited company — see converting a business name to a limited company.

What you'll need

  • Your company's CAC registration documents — certificate of incorporation, particulars of directors and shareholders, Memorandum and Articles of Association, and a board resolution authorising the application.
  • The company's tax identification number (TIN).
  • Company details — address, directors, contact person, and the products you intend to export.
  • Any other documents requested on the NEPC portal at the time you apply.

The process

  1. Create an account on NEPC's exporter e-registration portal. Registration is done online.
  2. Complete the application and upload the documents.
  3. Pay the registration fee. At the time of writing, NEPC lists ₦13,500 for a new certificate; check the portal for the current fee.
  4. Receive the certificate. NEPC states that certificates are issued within 24 hours once payment is confirmed and the documents are in order. Delays usually come from unconfirmed payments, incomplete information or documents that don't match.

Validity and renewal

2 yearsHow long a new NEPC certificate is valid. After that it is renewed every year — and renewing late costs more.

Set a reminder well before the expiry date. A lapsed certificate can hold up shipments and payments at the worst possible moment.

What else exporters usually need

NEPC registration is the foundation, not the whole picture. Depending on the product and destination, exporters also deal with:

  • Product certification — for example quality, health or phytosanitary certificates for food and agricultural products.
  • Product-specific rules — some goods have their own permits or are restricted for export.
  • The destination country's import rules, which your buyer can usually help confirm.

Can I export as a business name if I'm only shipping small quantities?

NEPC registration is only available to companies. Registering a limited company first is the reliable route — our packages start from ₦75,000, government fees included.

Can I register for NEPC from abroad?

Yes. The NEPC portal is online, and the company's documents can be prepared and submitted without you being in Nigeria.

Do I need to renew if I didn't export this year?

If you want to remain a registered exporter, yes. Renewing on time is cheaper than renewing late.

The short version

Register a company, get its TIN, apply on the NEPC portal, and you can usually have an exporter's certificate within a day of payment. Renew it on time, and plan for the product certificates your goods need.

After CAC approves a registration, you need two documents: the **certificate** and the **status report**. Banks, tax offices and clients ask for them. Here is where to get them.

## What each document is

| Document | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Certificate** | Proof that the business name, company or trustees is registered, with its BN, RC or IT number |
| **Status report** | The business's details on CAC's record, such as directors or proprietors and registered address |

Banks usually ask for both when you open an account. See [opening a business bank account](/blog/open-corporate-bank-account-nigeria/).

## Where to download them

CAC's registration portal FAQ says that once a business name shows as **Approved** on your dashboard, you can click **Action** and then **Download Certificate and Status Report**.

1. Log in to the account used for the application on CAC's registration portal (icrp.cac.gov.ng).
2. Open the application and check that its status is **Approved**.
3. Use the **Action** menu to download the certificate and status report.

::: callout
If the status is not "Approved", the documents are not ready yet. Do not pay anyone who promises to "release" them.
:::

If a consultant filed for you, the application may sit in their account. Ask them for copies, and ask for your login details if you want to manage the business yourself later.

## If you cannot see or download them

- Confirm the status is Approved, not "Pending" or "Query".
- Check that you are logged in to the right account.
- Try again later or from another browser, as portals are sometimes slow.
- Search for the business on CAC's public search to confirm it is registered. See [verifying a company](/blog/verify-company-cac-portal/).
- If it still does not appear, contact CAC through its official channels, or ask us.

## Why keep copies

Keep PDF copies in a safe place. You will need them for a [bank account](/blog/open-corporate-bank-account-nigeria/), [tax registration](/blog/tax-clearance-certificate-nigeria/), [SCUML](/blog/scuml-registration-nigeria/) and later changes. Also diarise your [annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/), because registration alone does not keep you in good standing. See [does CAC registration expire](/blog/does-cac-registration-expire/).

## The short version

When your registration shows as Approved on CAC's portal, download the certificate and status report from the Action menu on your dashboard. Keep copies, and if they will not appear, check the status and the account before contacting CAC.

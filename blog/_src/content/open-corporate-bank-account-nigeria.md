Once your business is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), the next step is usually a **business bank account**. Every bank has its own forms and checklist, but they all ask for broadly the same things, because they must verify who owns and controls the business under anti-money-laundering rules.

::: callout
Requirements differ between banks and change from time to time. Always ask your chosen bank for its **current checklist** before you visit, and ask whether any of it can be done online.
:::

## Business name account

Banks will usually ask for:

- **CAC certificate of registration** and the registration details (status report or application form showing the proprietors).
- **Valid ID** of each proprietor: international passport, driver's licence, voter's card or NIN slip.
- **BVN** of each proprietor.
- **Passport photographs.**
- **Proof of address**: a recent utility bill for the business address.
- **References**, often from existing account holders, on the bank's form.
- **Account opening forms** and a mandate card with signatories.

## Limited company account

Expect everything above, for **each director and signatory**, plus:

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Certificate of incorporation | Proves the company exists |
| Memorandum and articles of association | Shows the company's objects and powers |
| Status report or particulars of directors and shareholders | Shows who owns and controls the company |
| **Board resolution** to open the account | Authorises the account and names the signatories |
| IDs and BVNs of directors and signatories | KYC on the people behind the company |
| Details of shareholders with significant control | Beneficial-ownership checks |
| TIN | Often requested at opening or soon after. See [TIN retrieval](/blog/tin-retrieval-nigeria-2026/) |

Some banks also ask for **certified true copies** rather than ordinary copies. See [CAC certified true copies](/blog/cac-certified-true-copy/).

## Incorporated trustees (NGOs, churches, associations)

Banks will want the **certificate of incorporation**, the **constitution**, details and IDs of the **trustees**, a **resolution** of the trustees naming the signatories, and often a **SCUML certificate**. See [SCUML registration](/blog/scuml-registration-nigeria/).

## Why account openings stall

- **Names that don't match** across ID, BVN and CAC records.
- **Directors or signatories who aren't on the CAC record**: file changes first; see [adding or removing directors](/blog/add-remove-director-shareholder-cac/).
- **Missing board resolution**, or one that doesn't name the signatories.
- **References** not returned.
- **No proof of the business address.**
- **Foreign directors** without the residence or identity documents the bank requires.

## Opening from abroad

Many banks require signatories to complete identity checks, sometimes in person, before an account is activated. If you're outside Nigeria, ask the bank about remote onboarding, and consider naming a trusted signatory in Nigeria. See [choosing a local representative](/blog/local-representative-nigerian-business-abroad/).

<p class="blog-faq-q">Can I use my personal account for the business?</p>
<p class="blog-faq-a">You can for a business name, but a separate account makes records, tax and credibility far easier. For a company, it should have its own account in its exact registered name.</p>

<p class="blog-faq-q">Do I need a BVN to be a signatory?</p>
<p class="blog-faq-a">Nigerian banks generally require a BVN for every signatory. Ask your bank how it handles foreign signatories.</p>

<p class="blog-faq-q">How long does it take?</p>
<p class="blog-faq-a">It depends on the bank and how complete your documents are. Incomplete documents and missing references are the usual causes of delay.</p>

## The short version

Bring your CAC documents, every director's or proprietor's ID and BVN, proof of address, references and, for a company, a board resolution naming the signatories. Make sure names match everywhere and the CAC record is up to date.

Before you file a trademark in Nigeria, you should know whether someone else already owns the same or a confusingly similar mark. That's what an **availability search** is for, and it's the cheapest step in the whole process.

Skipping it is how businesses end up paying for an application that gets refused, opposed after publication, or worse, building a brand they later have to abandon.

## What the official search covers

Trademarks in Nigeria are registered by the **Trademarks Registry** under the Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment, through its online IP portal. An availability search checks the Registry's records for marks that are:

- **identical** to yours, or
- **similar enough to cause confusion**: similar spelling, sound or meaning,

in the **same class** of goods or services. Nigeria uses the international Nice classification, and each application covers one class, so the class you search is the class you intend to register. Our guide to [trademark classes](/blog/trademark-classes-explained-nigeria/) explains how to choose.

::: callout
A search is a snapshot. Applications filed shortly before yours may not appear yet, which is one reason to file promptly once the search comes back clear.
:::

## The checks worth doing alongside it

The Registry search doesn't show everything that could cause you trouble. Before committing to a name, also check:

- **CAC's company and business name register.** A company already trading under the name may have rights from use, even without a trademark.
- **The market.** Search online marketplaces, social media handles and Google for businesses using the name or something close to it.
- **Domain names and handles** you'll want to use.
- **Meaning and pronunciation** in major Nigerian languages, so the name doesn't carry an unfortunate meaning.

## How to read the results

| Result | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No identical or similar marks in your class | Good chance of acceptance | File promptly |
| Similar marks in a **different** class | Usually fine, unless the other mark is very well known | File, keeping your class description precise |
| Similar marks in the **same** class | Real risk of refusal or opposition | Adjust the name, add a distinctive element, or choose another |
| Identical mark in the same class | Very likely refused | Choose a different name |

## What happens after a clear search

Once you file, the application goes through **examination and acceptance**, then **publication in the Trade Marks Journal**, followed by a **two-month window** in which anyone can oppose it. If no one opposes, the certificate can be issued. A registration lasts **seven years** and can then be renewed. See [how much trademark registration costs](/blog/trademark-registration-cost-nigeria/) for what each stage involves.

::: quote
The search costs a fraction of a full application, and it's the step that tells you whether the application is worth making at all.
:::

<p class="blog-faq-q">Can I search by myself?</p>
<p class="blog-faq-a">You can do the informal checks (online, social media, CAC) yourself. The official availability search is done through the Registry's portal; we run it as part of every trademark engagement and explain the results.</p>

<div class="blog-faq-item">
<p class="blog-faq-q">My business name is registered with CAC. Do I still need a trademark search?</p>
<p class="blog-faq-a">Yes. CAC registration and trademark registration are separate systems: a name approved by CAC can still clash with someone's registered trademark. See <a href="/blog/business-name-vs-trademark-nigeria/">business name vs trademark</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="blog-faq-item">
<p class="blog-faq-q">Should I search my logo as well as my name?</p>
<p class="blog-faq-a">If you plan to register the logo, yes. Word marks and logo marks are assessed differently, and a distinctive logo can sometimes help a common word get through.</p>
</div>
</div>

## The short version

Search before you file, in the class you'll actually register. Combine the official Registry search with checks of CAC, the market and online handles, and file quickly once the coast is clear.

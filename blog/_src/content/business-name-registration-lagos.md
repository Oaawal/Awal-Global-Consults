Business names are registered nationally by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), so the process is the same in Lagos as anywhere in Nigeria. What changes in Lagos is what comes **after** registration: state tax, local permits and the documents banks ask for.

## What it costs

::: stat
₦40,000
Our business name registration package for Lagos businesses, government fees included. Usually ready within 24 hours of complete details.
:::

The official CAC fees inside that are ₦1,000 for the name and ₦20,000 for registration, plus Remita charges. See [business name registration cost](/blog/business-name-registration-cost-nigeria/) and [CAC fees 2026](/blog/cac-fees-2026-fee-table/).

## What you need

- Two or three proposed names
- A clear description of the business
- The business address in Lagos, with the LGA
- For each owner: full name, date of birth, address, phone, email, valid ID, passport photograph and signature

Full list: [business name document checklist](/blog/business-name-registration-document-checklist/).

## After registration in Lagos

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| **TIN and tax** | The owner pays personal income tax to the **Lagos State Internal Revenue Service (LIRS)** if they live in Lagos. See [how business names are taxed](/blog/business-name-partnership-company-tax-nigeria/) |
| **Bank account** | Banks ask for your certificate, ID, BVN and a recent utility bill. See [opening a business bank account](/blog/open-corporate-bank-account-nigeria/) |
| **Local permits** | Some local governments and Lagos State agencies require signage or premises permits. See [which licences you need](/blog/licences-new-business-nigeria/) |
| **Annual returns** | Business names file every year by 30 June, except the year of registration. See [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/) |

Many Nigerians in Canada keep a business running back home — a family trade, an import-export line, property, or a new venture they plan to grow. You can register it from Canada without travelling: the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registration is online and every document can be sent from abroad.

## Business name or company?

| | Business name | Limited company |
|---|---|---|
| Liability | Personal — business debts are yours | Limited to your shares |
| Best for | Small trading under your own name | Growth, staff, contracts, partners, exporting |
| Typical time (our experience) | Within 24 hours | 48–72 hours if no queries |
| Our packages from (government fees included) | ₦40,000 | ₦75,000 |

If you plan to **export from Nigeria**, choose a company: the Nigerian Export Promotion Council does not issue export registration to business names. See [export licence in Nigeria: NEPC registration explained](/blog/nepc-export-licence-nigeria/).

## Permanent resident or citizen: does it matter?

- **Permanent residents** are still Nigerian citizens, so you register with your Nigerian passport or NIN like anyone else.
- **Canadian citizens who kept Nigerian citizenship** can also register as Nigerians.
- **If you'll register a company using only a Canadian passport**, the company has **foreign participation** and needs a **minimum share capital of ₦100 million**. These registrations are quoted individually, and further registrations such as NIPC may apply.

## What to prepare

- An international passport (or NIN for Nigerian citizens). A provincial driver's licence may not be accepted.
- Your Canadian home address, for your personal details.
- A **business address in Nigeria** — see [do I need a Nigerian address?](/blog/nigerian-address-cac-registration/)
- A clear signature image and a passport photo.
- Two or three business name options and a clear description of the business.

## Signatures and notarised documents

A scanned specimen signature is normally enough for CAC registration. If a bank or another institution asks for notarised documents, they are usually **notarised by a Canadian notary and then authenticated by the Nigerian High Commission or a Nigerian consulate in Canada**. Ask the institution what it needs first. See [signing CAC documents from abroad](/blog/signing-cac-documents-from-abroad/).

## Canadian tax: a quick check

Canadian residents are generally taxed on worldwide income, and owning shares in a foreign company can bring **Canada Revenue Agency reporting requirements**. Take Canadian tax advice on your own situation before you register, especially if the business will be profitable or hold significant assets.

## The short version

From Canada, CAC registration is fully remote and usually done in one to three days. Register with your Nigerian passport if you can, choose a company if you'll export or grow, and check your Canadian tax reporting.

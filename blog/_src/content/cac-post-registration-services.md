After registration, your CAC record has to stay accurate. Banks, clients, regulators and courts rely on it. "Post-registration services" are the filings that keep it up to date.

## The services, and when you need them

| Service | When you need it | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual returns** | Every year | [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/) |
| **Change of directors** | Someone joins or leaves the board | [Adding or removing a director](/blog/add-remove-director-shareholder-cac/) |
| **Change of shareholders (share transfer)** | Shares are sold, gifted or inherited | [Adding or removing a shareholder](/blog/add-remove-director-shareholder-cac/) |
| **Allotment of shares / increase in share capital** | New shares are issued or capital is raised | [Share capital](/blog/share-capital-new-company-nigeria/) |
| **Change of address** | The registered office or business address moves | [Changes after registration](/blog/change-business-name-address-partners-cac/) |
| **Change of name** | You rebrand | [Changes after registration](/blog/change-business-name-address-partners-cac/) |
| **Change of partners or proprietors** (business names) | A partner joins or leaves | [Changes after registration](/blog/change-business-name-address-partners-cac/) |
| **Status report** | A bank, client or regulator wants current details | [Verifying a company](/blog/verify-company-cac-portal/) |
| **Certified true copies** | Courts, embassies, tenders and formal transactions | [Certified true copies](/blog/cac-certified-true-copy/) |
| **Persons with significant control** | Ownership or control changes | [Adding or removing a shareholder](/blog/add-remove-director-shareholder-cac/) |

::: callout
File changes **when they happen**, not when you next need a document. Company law sets short deadlines for most notices, late filings attract penalties, and a bank will refuse a signatory who isn't on the CAC record.
:::

## How post-registration filings work

1. **The decision is made properly**: for a company, usually a board or members' resolution.
2. **The documents are prepared**: forms, resolutions, share transfer instruments.
3. **The filing is made on the CAC portal**, with government fees paid through Remita.
4. **CAC updates the record**, and you can download an updated status report.

## Signs your record needs updating

- Your bank asks for a status report and the directors don't match.
- A partner or director has left, but is still on the record.
- You've moved, but the registered address is the old one.
- You've missed one or more annual returns. See [does CAC registration expire?](/blog/does-cac-registration-expire/)

## The short version

After registration, keep CAC's record accurate: file annual returns every year and file changes to directors, shareholders, address, name or share capital as they happen. Status reports and certified true copies prove your current details.

Your CAC registration is a public record of who owns your business, who runs it, where it is and what it does. When any of those change, **the record has to be updated** — and the law expects it to be done promptly.

Keeping it current isn't just paperwork. Banks, regulators, tenders and partners check CAC records, and a mismatch between what's filed and reality is one of the quickest ways to have an account frozen, a contract delayed or a TCC refused.

## Changes for business names

| Change | What's filed |
|---|---|
| New business address | Notice of change of address |
| Adding or removing a partner (proprietor) | Change of proprietors, with the incoming partner's details and ID |
| Change in the nature of the business | Change of business activity |
| Changing the name itself | A consent application first; the **name availability code** it produces is then used to file the change of name on CAC's post-registration portal |
| Closing the business | Notice of cessation |

## Changes for limited companies

| Change | What's usually needed |
|---|---|
| Registered office address | Board resolution, then notice to CAC |
| Appointing or removing a director | Board or members' resolution, the new director's details and consent, then notice to CAC |
| Transfer of shares | Share transfer documents, updated register of members, then notice to CAC |
| Increase in share capital | Members' resolution, then filing with CAC — and stamp duty on the increase |
| Change of company name | Special resolution, name availability check, CAC approval and a new certificate |
| Change of company secretary | Resolution and notice to CAC |

::: callout
Company law sets **short deadlines** for most of these notices, and late filings attract penalties. File as soon as the change is decided — not when you next need a document from CAC.
:::

## Changing your company name: what changes afterwards

A company that changes its name keeps the same RC number and remains the same legal person — its contracts, assets and liabilities continue. But you'll need to update:

- the **bank account name**,
- your **tax records**,
- **licences and permits** issued in the old name,
- **letterheads, invoices, website and signage**.

## Moving from a business name to a company

If the change you're planning is bigger — bringing in investors, limiting liability, or exporting — you may need a company rather than a change to your business name. See [can I convert my business name to a limited company?](/blog/convert-business-name-to-limited-company-nigeria/)

<p class="blog-faq-q">Can I change my business name's name without re-registering?</p>
<p class="blog-faq-a">Yes. You apply for consent for the new name, and CAC issues a name availability code that is used to file the change of name through its post-registration portal. Companies change their name by special resolution and keep the same RC number.</p>

<p class="blog-faq-a">Yes. Changes are filed when they happen. Your annual return then confirms the current position — see <a href="/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/">CAC annual returns</a>.</p>

## The short version

When your address, owners, directors, shares, activity or name change, file it with CAC promptly. Business names update proprietors, address and activity; companies need the right resolution first, then the CAC filing.

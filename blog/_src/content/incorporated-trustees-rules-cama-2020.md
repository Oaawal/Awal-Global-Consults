Once CAC registers your trustees, the association becomes a **body corporate** with perpetual succession, a common seal if it wants one, and the power to own property and to sue and be sued in its own name. All property held in trust for the association vests in it (CAMA 2020, s.830). With that comes a set of rules in **Part F of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020** (sections 823 to 850). Here they are in plain terms.

## Every year

| Duty | What the law says | Section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Annual returns** | File between **30 June and 31 December** each year, except the year of incorporation: name, trustees, council members, land held, and any constitution changes. | s.848(1) |
| **Audited accounts** | Each annual return must include the **audited statement of accounts** for the year. | s.848(2) |
| **Accounting records** | Keep day-to-day records of all money received and spent, and of assets and liabilities, enough to show the financial position at any time. | s.846 |
| **Keep records** | Preserve accounting records for **six years**. | s.847 |

Late or missing returns attract penalties set by CAC regulations (s.848(3)). See [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/) and the [compliance calendar](/blog/nigeria-business-compliance-calendar/).

## Money rules

- **Income and property go to the objects only.** Nothing may be paid to members as dividend, bonus or profit, directly or indirectly (s.838(1)). See [can incorporated trustees run a cooperative?](/blog/can-incorporated-trustees-run-a-cooperative-nigeria/)
- **Staff can be paid** reasonable remuneration for work actually done (s.838(2)).
- **Council members are restricted.** Apart from ex-officio members, members of the council or governing body cannot hold salaried or fee-paid posts in the organisation, and can only receive out-of-pocket expenses, reasonable rent for premises let to the organisation, or a reasonable fee for services actually rendered (s.838(2)).
- Anyone who knowingly misapplies income or property must **refund** it (s.838(3)).

## Changes need CAC approval and a public notice

| Change | Procedure | Section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name or objects | Resolution, application to CAC, publication in two newspapers, notice displayed at the headquarters for at least 28 days, objections window, then a new certificate for a new name | s.832 |
| Constitution | Resolution by simple majority of members, approved by CAC | s.833 |
| Replace or add trustees | Resolution at a general meeting, application to CAC, same publication and notice process | s.834 |

Any change made without following these steps is **void** (s.835). See [changing trustees, name or constitution](/blog/change-trustees-constitution-incorporated-trustees-nigeria/).

::: callout
Trustees must be qualified: not a minor, not of unsound mind as found by a court, not an undischarged bankrupt, and not convicted of fraud or dishonesty within the last five years. A disqualified bankrupt or fraud convict who acts as trustee faces a daily penalty (s.826).
:::

## CAC's supervisory powers

- **Suspension and interim managers (s.839).** Where there is misconduct, mismanagement or fraud, or it is needed to protect the association's property or the public interest, trustees can be suspended and interim managers appointed. The suspension is made **by court order** on a petition by CAC or by members making up **one fifth** of the association, and interim managers work under CAC's supervision. Excessive pay or administrative spending counts as mismanagement.
- **Dormant bank accounts (s.842).** If all the association's bank accounts become dormant, the bank must tell CAC. CAC may ask for evidence that the association is still active; if it does not respond satisfactorily within **15 days**, CAC may dissolve it and direct the bank to transfer the balance to other associations with similar objects.
- **Related associations (s.831).** CAC may treat an association as part of an already registered one, or treat associations with the same trustees as one.
- **Merger (s.849).** Associations with similar aims and objects may merge on terms CAC prescribes.

## Dissolution

A court can dissolve incorporated trustees on a petition by the council, a trustee, members making up at least half the membership, or CAC: for example when the objects are achieved, the set period has ended, the objects have become unlawful, or it is just and equitable. Remaining property **never** goes to members: it goes to an institution with similar objects chosen by the members, or otherwise to charity (s.850).

## Other duties outside CAMA

- **SCUML registration** for non-profit organisations. See [SCUML registration](/blog/scuml-registration-nigeria/).
- **Tax:** registration does not make an organisation tax-free automatically; trading income can be taxable. See [Nigeria Tax Act 2025](/blog/nigeria-tax-act-2025-compliance-guide/).
- **Data protection** if you hold members' or beneficiaries' personal data. See [NDPA compliance](/blog/ndpa-compliance-nigerian-business/).

## The short version

File annual returns between 30 June and 31 December with audited accounts, keep records for six years, never pay members a share of income, and get CAC approval (with a public notice) before changing name, objects, constitution or trustees. CAC can petition the court to suspend trustees for mismanagement and can dissolve associations whose accounts go dormant if they do not respond within 15 days.

Source: Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, Part F, sections 826 to 850.

The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) has announced plans to give registered businesses a free **.ng domain name**, a **business email address** and a **website**. CAC shared the plan in a teaser video on its official social media on Thursday 12 November 2026, as reported by MSME Africa on 14 November 2026.

It is a plan, not a launched service. Here is what we know, what we don't, and what makes sense now.

## What CAC announced

- Registered businesses could receive a free .ng domain name, a professional business email address and a website to show their products and services.
- The aim is to take the benefit of registration beyond the certificate, so small businesses can get online, look credible and reach more customers.
- CAC says it is still finalising partnerships to deliver it.

## What is not known yet

CAC has not announced:

- a launch date;
- who qualifies (for example, new registrations only, or existing businesses too; business names, companies, or both);
- how long the domain, email and website stay free, and what renewal will cost;
- where or how to apply.

CAC also confirmed the services are **not open for applications yet**.

::: callout
There is nothing to apply for yet. Ignore anyone offering to "register you for the CAC free website" for a fee. Watch CAC's official website and social media for the launch announcement.
:::

## Background: free .ng domains have been discussed before

The Nigeria Internet Registration Association (NiRA), which manages the .ng domain, has discussed free domains for newly registered businesses with CAC and NITDA in the past. Earlier proposals did not become a lasting programme, which is another reason to wait for CAC's official details before planning around this one.

## What to do now

1. **Make sure your business is registered with CAC.** The plan is for registered businesses, so an unregistered business is unlikely to qualify. See [how to register a business name step by step](/blog/register-business-name-nigeria-step-by-step/), and note that the [free SMEDAN business name registration](/blog/free-cac-business-name-registration-smedan/) has also reopened for eligible small businesses.
2. **Keep your CAC details up to date.** If your address, email or phone number has changed, update them, because launch details will likely be sent to the contacts on your registration. See [changes after registration](/blog/change-business-name-address-partners-cac/).
3. **Don't wait if you need a website now.** A plan with no date is not something to build a launch around. If you set one up yourself, protect the accounts from day one: see [securing your business email and social media](/blog/secure-business-email-social-media-accounts/).

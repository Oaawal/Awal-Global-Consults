Trademark registration in Nigeria is handled by the **Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry**. It's a multi-stage process, and the honest answer to "how long?" is: **commonly 18 to 24 months, and sometimes longer**. The good news is that once registered, your rights date back to the **filing date**.

## The stages

| Stage | What happens | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Search | Check whether similar marks are already registered or pending — see [trademark search](/blog/trademark-search-nigeria/) | Days to a few weeks |
| 2. Filing | Application filed in the right class — see [trademark classes](/blog/trademark-classes-explained-nigeria/) | Immediate; you get a filing date |
| 3. Acknowledgement | The Registry acknowledges the application | Soon after filing |
| 4. Examination and acceptance | The Registry checks the mark can be registered | Several months |
| 5. Publication | The accepted mark is published in the Trademarks Journal | Often the longest wait — the Journal isn't published on a fixed schedule |
| 6. Opposition | Anyone can oppose within **two months** of publication | Two months |
| 7. Registration | If unopposed, the registration fee is paid and the certificate issues | A few months |

::: callout
Publication in the Trademarks Journal is the usual bottleneck. Timelines depend on the Registry, not the applicant — so file as early as possible.
:::

## Can I use my mark while I wait?

Yes. You can use the mark, and you can show it as "TM". Don't use the **®** symbol until the mark is registered. Your application is also a public record that someone searching will find.

## How long protection lasts

::: stat
7 years
The first registration term, counted from the filing date. After that, each renewal lasts 14 years.
:::

- You can renew in the **three months before** the registration expires.
- Late renewal is possible within a limited window after the Registry gives notice of expiry, with a penalty fee.
- If you don't renew, the mark can be removed from the register — and someone else could register it. See [someone registered my name as a trademark](/blog/someone-registered-my-name-as-trademark/).

## What slows registration down

- **Objections at examination** — the mark is too descriptive or similar to an existing one.
- **Oppositions** after publication.
- **Wrong class or vague description** of goods and services.
- **Missing documents**, such as authorisation from the owner.

## The short version

Expect 18 to 24 months or more, mainly because of publication delays. File early, because protection runs from the filing date. The first term is 7 years, then 14 years per renewal — diary the date.

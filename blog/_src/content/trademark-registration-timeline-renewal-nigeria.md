Trademark registration in Nigeria is handled by the **Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry**. It's a multi-stage process. In our experience, the **final certificate usually takes 12 to 18 months** from filing. The good news is that once registered, your rights date back to the **filing date**.

## The stages and how long each takes

| Stage | What happens | Estimated timeline |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Search | Check whether similar marks are already registered or pending. See [trademark search](/blog/trademark-search-nigeria/) | **1–3 working days** |
| 2. Filing | Application filed in the right class. See [trademark classes](/blog/trademark-classes-explained-nigeria/) | You get a filing date |
| 3. Acknowledgement letter | The Registry acknowledges the application | **1–3 days** after filing |
| 4. Acceptance letter | The Registry examines the mark and accepts it. At this point your mark is partly considered approved | Typically **2–8 weeks**. For applications we handle, usually **within 5 working days** |
| 5. Publication | The accepted mark is published in the Trademarks Journal for **60 days**, during which anyone can oppose it | Waiting for publication can take a long time |
| 6. Final certificate | If unopposed, the Registry issues the official certificate | **12–18 months** from filing |

These are our estimates from experience, not official Registry timelines.

::: callout
Publication in the Trademarks Journal is the usual bottleneck. Timelines depend on the Registry, not the applicant, so file as early as possible, and keep your acceptance letter safe.
:::

## Can I use my mark while I wait?

Yes. You can use the mark, and you can show it as "TM". Don't use the **®** symbol until the mark is registered. Your application is also a public record that someone searching will find.

## How long protection lasts

::: stat
7 years
The first registration term, counted from the filing date. After that, each renewal lasts 14 years.
:::

- You can renew in the **three months before** the registration expires.
- Late renewal is possible within a limited window after the Registry gives notice of expiry, with a penalty fee.
- If you don't renew, the mark can be removed from the register, and someone else could register it. See [someone registered my name as a trademark](/blog/someone-registered-my-name-as-trademark/).

## What slows registration down

- **Objections at examination**: the mark is too descriptive or similar to an existing one.
- **Oppositions** after publication.
- **Wrong class or vague description** of goods and services.
- **Missing documents**, such as authorisation from the owner.

## The short version

Search takes 1–3 working days, acceptance typically 2–8 weeks (usually within 5 working days when we handle your application), and the final certificate usually 12–18 months, mainly because of publication delays. File early, because protection runs from the filing date. The first term is 7 years, then 14 years per renewal: diary the date.

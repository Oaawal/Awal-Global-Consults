A **business name** is the simplest way to trade legally in Nigeria under a name other than your own. It suits sole traders, freelancers, small shops and partnerships starting out. Registration is done online with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), and in our experience it's usually completed **within 24 hours** once your details are complete.

::: callout
A business name is not a separate legal person — you and the business are the same in law, so its debts are yours. If you need limited liability, investors or exports, see [business name vs limited company](/blog/business-name-vs-limited-company-nigeria/).
:::

## Who can register

- **Individuals** aged 18 or over, trading alone (sole proprietor) or with partners.
- **Partnerships** of two or more people trading under one name.
- Civil servants should read [can a civil servant register a business name?](/blog/can-civil-servant-register-business-name-nigeria/) first.

## What you need

- **Two or three proposed names**, in order of preference.
- **The nature of the business** — a clear description of what you'll do.
- **A business address in Nigeria** — see [do I need a Nigerian address?](/blog/nigerian-address-cac-registration/)
- For each proprietor: **full name, date of birth, nationality, occupation, phone, email and residential address**.
- For each proprietor: **valid ID** (international passport, NIN, or a Nigerian driver's licence or voter's card), a **passport photograph** and a **signature image**.
- The **commencement date** — when the business started or will start.

## The steps

1. **Name availability search.** The name is checked against existing registrations and restricted words. See [why CAC names get rejected](/blog/cac-name-search-rejection-reasons/).
2. **Name reservation.** The approved name is reserved for you for a limited period while registration is completed.
3. **Registration form.** Proprietor details, address and business activity are entered on the CAC portal.
4. **Upload documents** — ID, photograph and signature for each proprietor.
5. **Payment** of the registration fee.
6. **Review and approval** by CAC. Queries, if any, are answered at this stage.
7. **Certificate.** Once approved, the certificate and status report can be downloaded.

## After you register

- **Tax.** A business name owner is taxed personally. Keep records from day one — see [Nigeria Tax Act 2025](/blog/nigeria-tax-act-2025-compliance-guide/).
- **Bank account** in the business name — banks will ask for the certificate and status report.
- **Annual returns** every year, even in years you don't trade — see [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/).
- **Protect the brand.** A business name doesn't stop others using your brand — see [business name vs trademark](/blog/business-name-vs-trademark-nigeria/).

## The short version

Pick two or three names, prepare ID, photo and signature for each proprietor, give a Nigerian business address, and register online. It's usually done within a day — then open a bank account, keep tax records and file annual returns.

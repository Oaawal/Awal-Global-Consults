**No, a CAC certificate doesn't have an expiry date.** Once your business name, company or incorporated trustees is registered, the registration continues. But staying registered and staying **in good standing** are different things.

## What keeps your registration active

::: stat
Every year
Business names, companies and incorporated trustees must file annual returns with CAC, whether or not they traded.
:::

| Entity | Annual return deadline |
|---|---|
| Business name | By 30 June each year, except the year of registration |
| Company | Within 42 days of the AGM; the first AGM is within 18 months of incorporation |
| Incorporated trustees | Between 30 June and 31 December each year, except the year of incorporation |

See the [compliance calendar](/blog/nigeria-business-compliance-calendar/) for worked examples.

## What happens if you stop filing

- **Late filing penalties** build up for each year missed.
- **Your status can show as inactive** on CAC's register, which banks, clients and tender panels check. See [verifying a company](/blog/verify-company-cac-portal/).
- **Struck off:** CAC can remove businesses and companies that don't file for long periods. A struck-off company stops existing as a legal entity until it's restored.

::: callout
CAC has been running strike-off exercises against companies that haven't filed. If you're behind, catch up before your business appears on a strike-off list.
:::

## If your business is already inactive

You can usually bring it back by filing the outstanding returns and paying the penalties. If it has been struck off, restoration is a longer process. See [reviving an inactive company](/blog/revive-inactive-company-unfiled-annual-returns/).

## Other things that can "expire"

Your CAC registration doesn't expire, but some things around it do:

- **Licences and permits** often need yearly renewal. See [which licences you need](/blog/licences-new-business-nigeria/).
- **Tax clearance certificates** are issued for a year at a time. See [tax clearance certificates](/blog/tax-clearance-certificate-nigeria/).
- **Trademarks** last 7 years from filing, then 14 years per renewal. See [trademark timelines](/blog/trademark-registration-timeline-renewal-nigeria/).

## The short version

A CAC certificate doesn't expire, but your business stays in good standing only if you file annual returns every year. Missed returns lead to penalties, an inactive status and, eventually, being struck off.

For a small business, good advice is less about big strategy and more about **avoiding expensive mistakes early**: the wrong structure, an unclear partnership, missed filings or a licence you didn't know you needed.

## What business advisory usually covers

| Area | Typical questions |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | Business name or company? Who should own and run it? See [business name vs limited company](/blog/business-name-vs-limited-company-nigeria/) |
| **Tax set-up** | How will profits be taxed? Do we qualify as a small company? Do we need VAT? See [how each business type is taxed](/blog/business-name-partnership-company-tax-nigeria/) |
| **Licences and permits** | Which regulator covers us, and what must we have before trading? See [which licences you need](/blog/licences-new-business-nigeria/) |
| **Partners and shareholders** | How do we split ownership, and what happens if someone leaves? |
| **Contracts** | Are our client, supplier and employment agreements protecting us? |
| **Compliance** | What must we file each year, and when? See the [compliance calendar](/blog/nigeria-business-compliance-calendar/) |
| **Growth** | Raising capital, bringing in a foreign investor, expanding or exporting. See [NEPC export licence](/blog/nepc-export-licence-nigeria/) |

## When advice is worth paying for

- **Before you register**, if there are partners, a foreign shareholder or a regulated product.
- **Before you sign** a major contract, lease or investment.
- **When something changes:** a partner leaves, you take on investors, or you cross the ₦100 million VAT threshold.
- **When a regulator or bank asks** for something you don't understand.

::: callout
The cheapest time to get advice is before a decision is made. Restructuring a company, unwinding a partnership or catching up on missed filings costs far more than an hour of advice at the start.
:::

## How to choose an adviser

- **Relevant experience:** have they handled businesses like yours?
- **Clear scope and fee:** you should know what you're paying for before you start.
- **Plain answers:** good advisers explain what to do, not just what the law says.
- **Follow-through:** can they also handle the filings, or will you need someone else?

Directors run a company on behalf of its shareholders. Under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA 2020), most adults can be directors, but there are clear exceptions, and the role carries real legal duties.

## How many directors you need

| Company | Minimum directors |
|---|---|
| **Small company** | One |
| **Other private companies** | Two |
| **Public companies** | More, with rules on independent directors |

See [single-member companies](/blog/single-member-company-nigeria/) for one-person companies.

## Who can't be a director

- **Anyone under 18.** CAMA 2020 does not allow a minor to be a director.
- **People of unsound mind**, as found by a court.
- **Undischarged bankrupts**, unless the court permits.
- **People disqualified by a court**, for example after fraud or serious misconduct in managing a company.
- **People removed or barred** under the company's own articles or by law.

::: callout
CAC records who your directors are, and banks check them. Appointing someone who is disqualified, or listing a director who isn't really involved, can cause problems with your bank, contracts and liability later.
:::

## Can a foreigner be a director?

Yes. Foreign nationals can be directors of Nigerian companies. A foreign director who lives and works in Nigeria needs the right immigration status. If a foreigner also holds shares, foreign-ownership rules apply. See [can a foreigner own a Nigerian company?](/blog/foreigner-own-nigerian-company/) and [expatriate quota](/blog/expatriate-quota-how-many-foreign-staff-nigeria/).

## Can a civil servant be a director?

A full-time public officer generally must not take part in managing or running a private business, other than farming, under the Constitution's Code of Conduct. Being a director is managing the business. See [can a civil servant register a business?](/blog/can-civil-servant-register-business-name-nigeria/)

## What being a director means

- **Duties:** act in good faith in the company's interest, avoid conflicts of interest, use proper care and skill, and don't misuse company property or information.
- **Filings:** directors are responsible for keeping records and filing annual returns. See [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/).
- **Personal risk:** limited liability protects shareholders, but directors can be personally liable for some wrongdoing, such as fraud or trading recklessly.

## Changing directors later

Directors can be appointed or removed by the company and the change filed with CAC. See [adding or removing a director](/blog/add-remove-director-shareholder-cac/).

## The short version

Most adults can be directors. Minors, people of unsound mind, undischarged bankrupts and anyone disqualified by a court can't. Small companies need one director, others two. Choose directors who will really run the company, because the role carries legal duties.

Applications for the Federal Government's free Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) business name registration have reopened. Reports from Legit.ng (12 November 2026), Crisp Nigeria (13 November 2026) and MSME Africa (14 November 2026) say access to the application process through SMEDAN, the Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency of Nigeria, is open again.

If you run a small business that has never been registered with CAC, this is worth a look. Here is what it covers, who qualifies, and how to apply.

## What the programme is

The scheme started with a memorandum of understanding between SMEDAN and CAC, announced by SMEDAN on 27 September 2025, to register business names for **250,000 nano, micro and small businesses** without charging the registration fees. SMEDAN finds and profiles the businesses; CAC does the registration.

CAC said at the time that it would forgo about ₦3 billion in fees. Some recent posts mention a ₦6 billion figure: that is not a cash fund. The only benefit to you is a free business name registration, not a grant or payment.

::: callout
Nobody can sell you a guaranteed slot. The programme is free and applications go through SMEDAN's official portal (portal.smedan.gov.ng). Anyone asking for money to "secure" or "fast-track" your free registration is not part of it.
:::

## Who qualifies

According to the reports, the free registration is for:

- **Business names only:** sole proprietorships and partnerships. Limited companies, incorporated trustees and NGOs are not covered.
- **Businesses not yet registered with CAC.** It is not for renewing a certificate, changing a registered name or converting to a company. Businesses that have operated for years without registering can still apply.
- **Applicants with a valid NIN**, used to verify your identity.
- **A SMEDAN account.** If you already have one, use it rather than opening a new profile.

Applying does not guarantee approval. Slots are limited to 250,000 and no official figure shows how many are left.

## How to apply

1. Go to **portal.smedan.gov.ng** and create an account (name, email, password), then verify your email.
2. Complete your business profile: proposed business name, phone number, business address, state, local government area, year you started, what the business does, estimated turnover and number of staff. Say truthfully that the business is not registered with CAC.
3. Submit the profile. SMEDAN gives you a **SMEDAN Unique Identification Number (SUIN)**.
4. Follow the CAC registration instructions SMEDAN sends if your business is selected.
5. Once CAC approves the name, download your certificate and status report.

Your SUIN is not a CAC registration. You are only registered when CAC approves your business name and you have the certificate.

## When the free route is not the right one

The free scheme is a good deal if you have time and fit the rules. It may not suit you if:

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| You need the certificate soon (a bank account, contract or tender) | Selection and processing depend on the programme, with no fixed timeline |
| You want a limited company | Companies are not covered. See [business name vs limited company](/blog/business-name-vs-limited-company-nigeria/) |
| You are registering an NGO, church or association | Incorporated trustees are not covered |
| You are abroad without a working NIN set-up | The portal needs NIN verification. See [registering from the UK](/blog/register-business-nigeria-from-uk/) |
| You applied and were not selected | Slots are capped at 250,000 |

You can also register a business name yourself on CAC's portal. See [how to register a business name step by step](/blog/register-business-name-nigeria-step-by-step/) and [what business name registration costs](/blog/business-name-registration-cost-nigeria/).

## The short version

The FG and SMEDAN free business name registration for up to 250,000 small businesses has reopened. It covers unregistered sole proprietorships and partnerships with a valid NIN, and you apply through portal.smedan.gov.ng. It does not cover companies or NGOs, there is no guarantee of selection, and nobody can sell you a slot. If you need it fast or it does not fit your business, register through the normal CAC route.

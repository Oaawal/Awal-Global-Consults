A limited company is registered nationally by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), so the process is the same whether your office is in Ikeja, Lekki, Victoria Island, Yaba or Surulere. What differs in Lagos is what you do **after** registration.

## What it costs

::: stat
From ₦75,000
Our limited company package for Lagos businesses. Packages can be higher with add-ons, and every quote is itemised first.
:::

The official CAC charges are separate from our professional fee in a quote, so ask to see them itemised:

| Official charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| Name reservation | ₦1,000 + Remita |
| Submission | ₦10,000 per ₦1m share capital + Remita |
| Stamp duty | 0.75% of share capital (₦7,500 per ₦1m) + Remita |

See [CAC fees 2026](/blog/cac-fees-2026-fee-table/) and [share capital](/blog/share-capital-new-company-nigeria/).

## How long it takes

In our experience, a limited company takes **48 to 72 hours** if CAC raises no queries. This is our own experience, not an official CAC service time.

## What you need

- Two or three proposed company names
- The nature of the business
- A registered office address in Lagos
- Share capital and how it is split between shareholders
- For each director and shareholder: full name, date of birth, address, phone, email, valid ID (international passport, NIN, driver's licence or voter's card) and signature

Full list: [company registration document checklist](/blog/company-registration-document-checklist/). If you already trade under a business name, see [converting a business name to a limited company](/blog/convert-business-name-to-limited-company-nigeria/).

## After registration in Lagos

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| **Tax registration** | Register with the Nigeria Revenue Service for company tax, and with the Lagos State Internal Revenue Service (LIRS) for staff PAYE. See [the Nigeria Tax Act guide](/blog/nigeria-tax-act-2025-compliance-guide/) |
| **Bank account** | Banks ask for your certificate, ID, BVN and a utility bill for proof of address. See [opening a corporate account](/blog/open-corporate-bank-account-nigeria/) |
| **Licences and permits** | Some businesses need sector licences or local permits. See [which licences you need](/blog/licences-new-business-nigeria/) |
| **Annual returns** | Due within 42 days of the AGM; the first AGM is within 18 months of incorporation. See [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/) |

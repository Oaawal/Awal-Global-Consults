There's **no fixed number** of foreign staff a Nigerian company can employ. Instead, every foreign employee must fill a specific, **approved expatriate quota position** granted to the company by the Federal Ministry of Interior. The number of positions you get depends on what you can justify.

See [expatriate quota and CERPAC](/blog/cerpac-expatriate-quota-nigeria/) for how quota and the individual's residence permit fit together.

## How quota positions work

- A quota is granted **to the company**, for **named roles** — for example "Chief Engineer" or "Finance Director".
- Each approved position can be filled by **one foreign national** at a time.
- Positions are granted for a **period**, and must be renewed.
- Hiring a foreigner into a role that isn't approved — or more foreigners than approved positions — isn't allowed.

## What the Ministry looks at

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| The company's registration and capital | Shows the company is real and substantial |
| The nature of the business | Some sectors genuinely need specialist foreign skills |
| Why each role needs a foreign national | Generic justifications are a common reason for refusal |
| Nigerian staff and training plans | The policy aims to transfer skills to Nigerians |
| Tax and compliance record | A compliant company is a stronger applicant |
| How existing quota positions are used | Unused or misused positions weaken new requests |

::: callout
The expatriate quota system expects **Nigerian understudies**: Nigerian staff trained to take over the role in time. Be ready to show a real training and succession plan.
:::

## Permanent quota ("until reviewed")

Some senior positions — typically for the company's top management where the business is foreign-owned — can be granted on a longer-term basis, often described as "permanent until reviewed". Other positions are time-limited.

## Renewals and changes

- **Renew before expiry.** A lapsed quota leaves the employee's CERPAC without a valid position — see [CERPAC renewal](/blog/cerpac-renewal-nigeria/).
- **Apply for upgrades or additional positions** when the business grows.
- **Redesignate** a position if the role changes.
- **Report** when an expatriate leaves and the position is vacant.

The Ministry's **Expatriate Administration System** handles quota and related applications online.

## The short version

There's no fixed headcount. Each foreign employee needs an approved quota position, granted for named roles based on genuine need, the company's substance and plans to train Nigerians. Track expiry dates and renew on time.

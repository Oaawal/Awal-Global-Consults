Churches, mosques, fellowships and ministries in Nigeria register with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) as **incorporated trustees** under Part F of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020. CAMA expressly covers communities bound together by **religion** and associations established for **religious** purposes (s.823). The registered name takes the form **"The Incorporated Trustees of …"** (s.825).

The steps and timeline are in [how to register an NGO, church or association](/blog/register-ngo-church-incorporated-trustees-nigeria/): name consent, a 28-day public notice in two newspapers, sworn trustee declarations and final approval, usually five to six weeks. This guide covers the decisions that matter most for religious bodies.

## 1. Choosing the trustees

You need **at least two**, appointed by the congregation or governing body at a meeting whose minutes (with attendance and votes) go with the application (s.825). Trustees must not be minors, of unsound mind as found by a court, undischarged bankrupts, or convicted of fraud or dishonesty within five years (s.826).

Good practice for religious bodies:

- **More than two.** Three to seven trustees makes the body resilient if one dies, relocates or falls out.
- **Not only family.** A board made up of the founder's household is common and is a frequent source of disputes after the founder dies.
- **Clear terms.** The constitution should state how long trustees serve and how they are replaced. Replacing trustees later needs a general meeting resolution and CAC approval with a public notice (s.834).

## 2. A constitution that prevents disputes

CAMA requires the constitution to cover the name, aims and objects, the appointment, powers, duties, tenure and replacement of trustees, use of the seal, meetings, the governing body, and **how funds are collected, spent, accounted for and audited** (s.827). For churches and mosques, also settle:

- **Doctrine and affiliation:** the statement of faith or school of thought, and whether the body belongs to a wider denomination or council.
- **Succession:** how the general overseer, senior pastor, chief imam or leader is appointed, and what happens on death, retirement or misconduct.
- **Discipline and disputes:** how members or ministers are disciplined, and an internal dispute process before anyone goes to court.
- **Money:** who can sign cheques (at least two signatories), how offerings, tithes and zakat are counted and banked, and the annual audit.
- **Property:** that all land, buildings and vehicles are held in the name of the incorporated trustees.
- **Branches:** how branches and parishes are opened, run and closed, and that they operate under the one registration.

::: callout
Changing the constitution later needs a resolution by a simple majority of members **and** CAC approval (s.833). Changing the name or objects needs a public notice and a 28-day objection window (s.832). Get it right before you file.
:::

## 3. Property: get it out of individual names

Many churches and mosques have land registered in a founder's or pastor's personal name. Registration as incorporated trustees **vests in the body all property held by any person in trust for the association** (s.830(2)), but title documents should still be updated so the land is in the name of "The Incorporated Trustees of …". That protects the property if the individual dies, leaves or is sued personally.

## 4. Branches and parishes

One registration can cover a ministry and its branches if the constitution provides for them. Avoid registering each branch separately with different trustees unless they are meant to be independent: CAC may treat associations with the same trustees, or a branch, as part of an already registered association (s.831).

## 5. After registration

- **Bank account** in the registered name, with the signatories the constitution names. See [opening a corporate bank account](/blog/open-corporate-bank-account-nigeria/).
- **Annual returns** between 30 June and 31 December with audited accounts, and records kept for six years (s.846 to s.848). See [incorporated trustees rules under CAMA 2020](/blog/incorporated-trustees-rules-cama-2020/).
- **SCUML registration** as a non-profit organisation. See [SCUML registration](/blog/scuml-registration-nigeria/).
- **No profit to members:** offerings and income go to the ministry's objects only (s.838). Members' savings or loan schemes belong in a separate cooperative. See [can incorporated trustees run a cooperative?](/blog/can-incorporated-trustees-run-a-cooperative-nigeria/)
- **Tax:** income from trading activities (a school, a bookshop, rentals) may be taxable even when offerings are not. See [Nigeria Tax Act 2025](/blog/nigeria-tax-act-2025-compliance-guide/).

## The short version

Churches, mosques and ministries register with CAC as incorporated trustees. Pick at least three qualified trustees who are not all family, write a constitution that covers succession, money, discipline, property and branches, and move all property into the name of the incorporated trustees. Then file annual returns with audited accounts, register with SCUML, and keep members' savings schemes in a separate cooperative.

Source: Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, Part F, sections 823 to 850.

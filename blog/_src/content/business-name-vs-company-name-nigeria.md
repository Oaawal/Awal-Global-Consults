In everyday speech, "business name" just means what your business is called. At the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), it means something more specific, and that's where the confusion starts.

## Three different things

| Term | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Business name (registration)** | A type of registration for sole traders and partnerships. The business is not a separate legal person | "Ada Fashion House" registered as a business name |
| **Company name** | The registered name of a limited company, a separate legal person | "Ada Fashion House Limited" |
| **Trading name or brand** | The name customers see. It may differ from the registered name | A company called "AFH Holdings Limited" trading as "Ada Fashion" |

::: callout
A company's name must end with **"Limited"** or **"Ltd"** (or "Plc" for public companies). A business name doesn't have this, which is one quick way to tell them apart.
:::

## Why the difference matters

- **Liability:** with a business name, you are personally liable for its debts. With a company, liability is generally limited to your shares. See [business name vs limited company](/blog/business-name-vs-limited-company-nigeria/).
- **Bank accounts:** the account must be in the exact registered name.
- **Contracts and invoices:** use the registered name, and the trading name if different.
- **Brand protection:** neither registration stops others using your brand in trade. A trademark does. See [business name vs trademark](/blog/business-name-vs-trademark-nigeria/).

## Can a company use a different trading name?

Yes. A company can trade under another name, for example a shop or product brand. If you want that name protected and recognised, register it as a trademark, and make sure contracts and invoices still show the company's registered name.

## Can I change one into the other?

You can't convert a business name into a company directly, but you can keep the name and move the business into a new company. See [converting a business name to a limited company](/blog/convert-business-name-to-limited-company-nigeria/).

## The short version

A business name is a registration type for sole traders and partnerships; a company name belongs to a limited company and ends in "Limited". Your trading name can be different from both, and only a trademark protects your brand.

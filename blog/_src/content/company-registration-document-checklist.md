Registering a private company limited by shares means making a few **decisions** as well as gathering **documents**. Use this checklist before you start.

::: callout
Decide the share capital and who owns what **before** you file. These are the hardest things to change afterwards. See [how much share capital to register](/blog/share-capital-new-company-nigeria/).
:::

## Decisions to make first

- [ ] **Two or three proposed names**, ending in "Limited" or "Ltd". See [why CAC names get rejected](/blog/cac-name-search-rejection-reasons/)
- [ ] **Business objects**: a clear description of what the company will do
- [ ] **Registered office address in Nigeria**, with state and LGA. See [do I need a Nigerian address?](/blog/nigerian-address-cac-registration/)
- [ ] **Share capital**: at least ₦100,000, or ₦100 million with any foreign shareholder. See [CAC fees 2026](/blog/cac-fees-2026-fee-table/) for how it affects government fees
- [ ] **Shareholding**: how many shares each shareholder holds
- [ ] **Directors**: at least one for a small company, two for others. See [single-member companies](/blog/single-member-company-nigeria/)
- [ ] **Company secretary**, if the company isn't a small company
- [ ] **Persons with significant control**: worked out from the shareholders and their shareholding, so get the shareholding right first
- [ ] **Company email and phone number**

## For each director and shareholder

- [ ] **Full name**, exactly as on ID
- [ ] **Date of birth**
- [ ] **Gender and nationality**
- [ ] **Occupation**
- [ ] **Residential and service address**
- [ ] **Phone number and email address**
- [ ] **Valid ID**: international passport, NIN, Nigerian driver's licence or voter's card
- [ ] **Recent passport photograph** (clear digital copy)
- [ ] **Signature**: on plain white paper, clearly scanned or photographed
- [ ] **Number of shares** (for shareholders)

## Extra items in some cases

| Situation | What you'll also need |
|---|---|
| Any foreign shareholder | Their passport, at least ₦100m share capital, and later NIPC registration and other foreign-investment steps. See [can a foreigner own a Nigerian company?](/blog/foreigner-own-nigerian-company/) |
| A company as shareholder | Its certificate of incorporation, details of its directors, and a resolution appointing its representative |
| Directors or shareholders abroad | International passport and remote signing. See [signing CAC documents from abroad](/blog/signing-cac-documents-from-abroad/) |
| Regulated business | Check sector licences and any capital requirements first. See [which licences you need](/blog/licences-new-business-nigeria/) |
| Converting a business name | Your business name details. See [converting a business name to a company](/blog/convert-business-name-to-limited-company-nigeria/) |

## Before you submit: quick check

- [ ] Names, dates and ID numbers match on every document
- [ ] Shareholdings add up to the total share capital
- [ ] Every director and shareholder has ID, photograph and signature ready
- [ ] The objects cover everything the company will do
- [ ] Partners have agreed what happens if someone leaves: consider a shareholders' agreement

## For tax registration and your bank account

- [ ] **Recent utility bill for the business address**: needed as proof of address for tax registration with the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) and when opening your bank account

## After registration

Keep safe: the **certificate of incorporation**, **memorandum and articles of association**, **status report**, and **payment receipts**. You'll need them for your bank account, TIN, licences and annual returns. See [opening a corporate bank account](/blog/open-corporate-bank-account-nigeria/) and [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/).

## The short version

Decide your name, objects, address, share capital, shareholding and directors first. Then gather each director's and shareholder's details, ID, photograph and signature: all matching exactly. Foreign or corporate shareholders need extra documents.

Your **National Identification Number (NIN)** has become the key to almost everything official in Nigeria — passport applications and renewals, bank accounts, SIM registration, and many government services. If you live abroad and don't have one, you can enrol without travelling home.

::: callout
**You must attend in person.** NIN enrolment captures your fingerprints, photograph and signature, so you need to visit an approved enrolment centre yourself. No one can enrol on your behalf.
:::

## How diaspora enrolment works

The National Identity Management Commission (NIMC) licenses **diaspora enrolment partners** in many countries — NIMC reports enrolment centres in over 70 countries. These partners capture your details and biometrics and send them to NIMC for processing.

1. **Find an approved partner** in your country. Use only partners licensed by NIMC — check NIMC's official channels.
2. **Book an appointment** with the partner.
3. **Pay the enrolment fee** to the partner. Fees are set by the partner and vary by country.
4. **Attend in person** with your documents for biometric capture.
5. **Receive your NIN** once NIMC has processed your enrolment, then keep a record of your NIN slip or details.

## What to bring

- **Proof that you're Nigerian** — usually your Nigerian passport (current or expired). If you don't have one, ask the partner what other evidence of citizenship it accepts, such as a Nigerian birth certificate or a parent's documents.
- **A valid ID** from your country of residence.
- **Proof of address** where you live, if the partner asks for it.
- **Your details exactly as you want them recorded** — check spellings and your date of birth carefully.

::: quote
The details on your NIN will follow you into your passport, bank and other records. Get them right at enrolment — correcting a NIN later is much slower.
:::

## NIN for children

Children born abroad to Nigerian parents can also be enrolled. Partners will usually ask for the child's birth certificate and a parent's Nigerian passport or NIN.

## If you already enrolled but lost your NIN

You don't need to enrol again. Your NIN can be retrieved — see [NIN retrieval and correction](/blog/nin-retrieval-correction-nigeria/).

## The short version

Book with a NIMC-licensed diaspora partner, take proof of Nigerian citizenship and your local ID, attend in person for biometrics, and check every detail before you leave. Get your NIN before you start a passport renewal.

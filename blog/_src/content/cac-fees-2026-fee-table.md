These are the **official government fees** paid to the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), as charged on the CAC portal in 2026. They are paid through **Remita**, which adds its own processing charge to each payment.

::: callout
These are **government fees only**, not our package prices. Our packages (business name from ₦40,000, company from ₦75,000) already **include** the government fees for a standard registration. See [CAC registration cost](/blog/cac-registration-cost-nigeria/) for everything that goes into a total price.
:::

## Business name fees

| Item | Official fee |
|---|---|
| Name reservation | **₦1,000** + Remita charge |
| Registration (submission) | **₦20,000** + Remita charge |
| **Total government fees** | **₦21,000** + Remita charges |

## Company (limited by shares) fees

| Item | Official fee |
|---|---|
| Name reservation | **₦1,000** + Remita charge |
| Registration (submission) | **₦10,000 for every ₦1 million** of share capital + Remita charge |
| Stamp duty on share capital | **0.75%** of share capital (₦7,500 for every ₦1 million) + Remita charge |

## Worked examples for companies

| Share capital | Name reservation | Registration | Stamp duty | Total government fees (before Remita charges) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₦1,000,000 | ₦1,000 | ₦10,000 | ₦7,500 | **₦18,500** |
| ₦2,000,000 | ₦1,000 | ₦20,000 | ₦15,000 | **₦36,000** |
| ₦5,000,000 | ₦1,000 | ₦50,000 | ₦37,500 | **₦88,500** |
| ₦10,000,000 | ₦1,000 | ₦100,000 | ₦75,000 | **₦176,000** |
| ₦100,000,000 (minimum with a foreign shareholder) | ₦1,000 | ₦1,000,000 | ₦750,000 | **₦1,751,000** |

For share capital below ₦1 million, ask us for the exact figure.

::: stat
₦17,500
The extra government fees for every additional ₦1 million of share capital: ₦10,000 registration plus ₦7,500 stamp duty.
:::

That's why share capital is worth choosing carefully. See [how much share capital to register](/blog/share-capital-new-company-nigeria/) and, if a foreigner will hold shares, [can a foreigner own a Nigerian company?](/blog/foreigner-own-nigerian-company/)

## What these fees don't cover

- **Remita processing charges** on each payment.
- **Professional fees** for preparing documents, filing and handling queries.
- **Post-registration filings**: annual returns, changes of directors or address, increases in share capital. See [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/).
- **Other registrations**: TIN, SCUML, licences. See [which licences you need](/blog/licences-new-business-nigeria/).

## Fees can change

CAC fees are set by government and change from time to time. This table reflects the fees charged on the CAC portal as of **31 October 2026**. We update it when CAC changes its fees.

Most attacks on Nigerian small businesses aren't sophisticated. They target the easy gaps: a reused password, a WhatsApp account without a PIN, a staff member who pays a fake invoice. Close those gaps and you stop most of the risk.

::: callout
Work through this list with whoever manages your accounts and payments. Anything you can't tick is a gap to fix, starting with accounts and payments.
:::

## 1. Accounts and passwords

- [ ] **Two-step verification is on** for business email, WhatsApp Business, Instagram, Facebook and online banking
- [ ] **Every account has its own strong password**, never reused across accounts
- [ ] **Passwords are stored in a password manager**, not on paper or in a WhatsApp chat
- [ ] **Recovery email and phone number** on each account are ones the business controls
- [ ] **Former staff have been removed** from every account and shared login

## 2. WhatsApp Business

- [ ] **Two-step verification PIN is set**, with a recovery email
- [ ] **No one in the business ever shares a verification code**, whoever asks
- [ ] **Linked devices are checked** regularly and unknown ones removed

See [WhatsApp Business account security](/blog/whatsapp-business-account-security-nigeria/).

## 3. Payments

- [ ] **Any change to a supplier's bank details is confirmed by phone**, on a number you already had
- [ ] **Large or unusual payments need a second approval**
- [ ] **Staff know that urgency is a warning sign**, especially from "the boss" or a supplier
- [ ] **Bank alerts are switched on** for every business account

See [how businesses get scammed out of payments](/blog/business-email-compromise-payment-fraud-nigeria/).

## 4. Devices and Wi-Fi

- [ ] **Phones and laptops have a screen lock** and automatic updates
- [ ] **Business devices can be located and wiped** if lost
- [ ] **The office Wi-Fi password has been changed** from the default, with a separate network for visitors

## 5. Backups

- [ ] **Important files are backed up automatically**, to a cloud service or an external drive
- [ ] **You've tested restoring a file** in the last six months

## 6. Staff

- [ ] **Everyone knows how to spot phishing** and who to tell
- [ ] **New staff get a short security briefing**, and leavers lose access the same day
- [ ] **There's a simple written rule** for passwords, devices and payments

## 7. Customer data

- [ ] **You know what customer data you hold** and where it is
- [ ] **Only the people who need it can see it**
- [ ] **You have a privacy notice** and a plan for a data breach. See [does my business need a privacy policy?](/blog/privacy-policy-small-business-nigeria/) and [responding to a data breach](/blog/data-breach-response-nigeria-ndpa/)

## The short version

Turn on two-step verification, use a password manager, protect WhatsApp with a PIN, confirm bank-detail changes by phone, back up your files, brief your staff and know what customer data you hold. That covers most of the risk for most small businesses.

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (NTA 2025), in force from 2026, gives **small companies** a **0% company income tax (CIT) rate**. It's one of the most valuable reliefs for Nigerian businesses — but it has conditions, exclusions and filing duties that are easy to miss.

## The two-part test

A company is a **small company** only if **both** of these are true:

::: stat
₦100m
Annual gross turnover of **₦100 million or less**, and total fixed assets of **less than ₦250 million**.
:::

Fail either test and the company is not a small company for that year.

## Who is excluded

**Professional services businesses** are excluded **whatever their size**. That generally covers firms providing professional services such as legal, accounting, consulting, engineering and similar advisory work. A two-person consultancy with ₦20 million turnover still pays company income tax.

## What small companies don't pay

| Tax | Small company |
|---|---|
| Company income tax | **0%** |
| Capital gains tax | Exempt |
| Development levy (4%) | Exempt |

## What small companies still have to do

- **File annual tax returns** — a 0% rate is not an exemption from filing, and a nil return is required by law. Missing one attracts ₦100,000 for the first month and ₦50,000 for each month after. See [no profit — do I still file?](/blog/no-profit-still-file-tax-returns-nigeria/)
- **Deduct and remit PAYE** on staff salaries.
- **Withholding tax** obligations on qualifying payments, where they apply.
- **VAT** rules — see [VAT for small businesses](/blog/vat-registration-small-business-nigeria/).
- **CAC annual returns** — see [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/).

::: callout
Keep proper accounts. Your turnover and fixed-asset figures are what prove you qualify. Without records, you can't show the tax authority you're a small company.
:::

## What if you grow past the limits?

Once turnover exceeds ₦100 million, or fixed assets reach ₦250 million, the company is taxed at the standard rate for that year. Plan ahead: if you're approaching the threshold, speak to an accountant about the timing of large contracts and asset purchases — and make sure your accounts are ready.

## Business names

A business name isn't a company, so this exemption doesn't apply. The owner pays personal income tax on the business's profits to their state tax authority. See [the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 compliance guide](/blog/nigeria-tax-act-2025-compliance-guide/).

## The short version

Under the NTA 2025, a company with turnover of ₦100 million or less and fixed assets under ₦250 million pays 0% company income tax, capital gains tax and development levy — unless it provides professional services. It must still file every year.

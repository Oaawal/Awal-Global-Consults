Under CAMA 2020, incorporated trustees cannot simply update their records when leadership or plans change. Each change has its own procedure, and a change made outside it is **void** (s.835). Here is what each one involves.

## Replacing or adding trustees (s.834)

1. **Hold a general meeting** and pass a resolution replacing some or all trustees, or appointing additional ones, as the constitution provides.
2. **Apply to CAC** in the prescribed form, with the resolution and the new trustees' details and declarations.
3. **Public notice:** CAC has the application published in two daily newspapers (at least one national) and directs the association to display a notice at its headquarters or where most members will see it for **at least 28 days**.
4. **Objections** may be sent to CAC within 28 days of the last newspaper publication.
5. **CAC's written assent.** The appointment then takes effect **from the date of the resolution**.

New trustees must meet the qualification rules: not a minor, not of unsound mind as found by a court, not an undischarged bankrupt, not convicted of fraud or dishonesty within five years (s.826).

::: callout
When a trustee dies or leaves, act promptly. Banks and land registries deal with the trustees on CAC's record, so an outdated list can freeze accounts and property transactions.
:::

## Changing the name or objects (s.832)

1. **Resolution** approving the change, certified by the trustees.
2. **Application to CAC** setting out the change, with the resolution.
3. CAC checks the change is lawful, then requires **publication in two daily newspapers** and a **28-day notice** displayed at the headquarters or branches.
4. **Objections** within 28 days of the last publication.
5. If CAC assents, the change is made. For a **new name**, CAC issues a **new certificate**.

The new objects must still be religious, educational, literary, scientific, social, development, cultural, sporting or charitable (s.825, s.823). Objects about savings, loans or profit for members are not allowed; see [can incorporated trustees run a cooperative?](/blog/can-incorporated-trustees-run-a-cooperative-nigeria/)

## Amending the constitution (s.833)

1. **Resolution passed by a simple majority of members** (follow any stricter procedure your constitution sets).
2. **CAC approval** of the amended constitution. Section 833 makes this subject to sections 827 and 828, so CAC can require the same newspaper publication and objection window as a new registration.
3. The amended constitution must still cover what s.827 requires: trustees, seal, meetings, governing body, and how funds are collected, spent, accounted for and audited.

Constitution changes also go into the next **annual return** (s.848).

## Practical checklist

- Minutes showing the meeting was properly called, who attended and how they voted
- The resolution, signed and certified by the trustees
- New trustees' IDs, photographs, addresses, occupations and sworn declarations
- The amended constitution (clean copy) where relevant
- Board resolution for the bank to update signatories once CAC approves

## How long it takes

Any change that goes through newspaper publication cannot be approved until the **28-day objection window** after the last publication has closed, plus CAC's own processing time before and after it. Plan accordingly, and start as soon as the meeting has passed the resolution.

## The short version

Replacing trustees (s.834) and changing the name or objects (s.832) need a resolution, an application to CAC, publication in two newspapers, a 28-day notice and CAC's assent. Amending the constitution needs a simple-majority resolution and CAC approval (s.833). Skip the steps and the change is void (s.835).

Source: Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, Part F, sections 823 to 835 and 848.

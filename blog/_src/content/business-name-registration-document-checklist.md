Most delays in business name registration come from **missing or mismatched details**, not from CAC. Use this checklist before you start — whether you're registering yourself or sending your details to us.

::: callout
Make sure every name, date of birth and ID number matches **exactly** across your ID and the form. Mismatches are the most common cause of queries.
:::

## About the business

- [ ] **Two or three proposed names**, in order of preference — see [why CAC names get rejected](/blog/cac-name-search-rejection-reasons/)
- [ ] **Nature of business** — a clear, specific description of what you'll do
- [ ] **Business address in Nigeria**, with state and LGA — see [do I need a Nigerian address?](/blog/nigerian-address-cac-registration/)
- [ ] **Commencement date** — when the business started or will start
- [ ] **Business email and phone number**

## For each proprietor or partner

- [ ] **Full name** — surname, first name and other names, exactly as on ID
- [ ] **Date of birth** — proprietors must be 18 or over
- [ ] **Gender and nationality**
- [ ] **Occupation**
- [ ] **Residential address**
- [ ] **Phone number and email address**
- [ ] **Valid ID** — international passport, NIN, Nigerian driver's licence or voter's card
- [ ] **Recent passport photograph** (clear digital copy)
- [ ] **Signature** — signed on plain white paper, scanned or photographed clearly

## Extra items in some cases

| Situation | What you'll also need |
|---|---|
| Registering from abroad | Your international passport is the safest ID — see [registering from the UK](/blog/register-business-nigeria-from-uk/) and [signing documents from abroad](/blog/signing-cac-documents-from-abroad/) |
| You're a civil servant | Check your service rules first — see [can a civil servant register a business name?](/blog/can-civil-servant-register-business-name-nigeria/) |
| A proprietor is under 18 | A minor can't be a proprietor — register in an adult's name |
| The name includes a restricted word | Supporting approval from the relevant body may be needed |
| A partnership | The details above for **every** partner |

## Before you submit — quick check

- [ ] Names, dates and ID numbers match on every document
- [ ] Photographs and signatures are clear, not blurred or cropped
- [ ] The business description matches what you'll actually do
- [ ] The address is complete and in Nigeria

## After registration

Keep these safe: your **certificate**, **registration details or status report**, and **payment receipts**. You'll need them for your bank account, TIN and annual returns — see [opening a business bank account](/blog/open-corporate-bank-account-nigeria/) and [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/).

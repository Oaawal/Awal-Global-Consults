The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registers business names, companies and incorporated trustees **online**. Whether you file yourself or use an agent, the application goes through the same portal, and the certificate is issued electronically.

## What you do on the portal

1. **Create an account** with your email and phone number.
2. **Search and reserve a name.** You submit one or more proposed names and wait for approval. See [why names get rejected](/blog/cac-name-search-rejection-reasons/).
3. **Fill the registration form** for the type of entity: business name, company or incorporated trustees.
4. **Upload documents:** ID, passport photograph and signature for each proprietor, director or shareholder, and any supporting documents.
5. **Pay** the government fees through Remita, which adds a small processing charge. See [CAC fees 2026](/blog/cac-fees-2026-fee-table/).
6. **Submit and respond to queries.** CAC checks the application and may raise a query if something doesn't match.
7. **Download your certificate** and registration documents once approved.

::: callout
Most delays come from details that don't match: a name spelt differently on the ID and the form, a blurred photograph, or a vague description of the business. Check everything before you pay.
:::

## What you'll need ready

- For a company: see the [company registration checklist](/blog/company-registration-document-checklist/).

## How long it takes

In our experience, a business name is usually approved within 24 hours of complete details, and a company within 48 to 72 hours if CAC raises no queries. See [how long CAC registration takes](/blog/how-long-cac-registration-takes/).

## Doing it yourself or using an agent

You don't need a lawyer to register. Doing it yourself saves the service fee; an agent saves time, avoids queries and gets the structure right. See [do I need a lawyer to register a company?](/blog/do-i-need-lawyer-register-company-nigeria/)

## After approval

Registration is the start. Next come your TIN, bank account and any licences, then yearly annual returns. See [CAC post-registration services](/blog/cac-post-registration-services/).

## The short version

CAC registration is fully online: reserve a name, complete the form, upload documents, pay through Remita, answer any queries and download your certificate. Matching details and clear documents are what keep it fast.

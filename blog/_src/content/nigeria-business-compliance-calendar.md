Most penalties Nigerian businesses pay aren't for doing anything wrong: they're for **filing late**. This calendar puts every recurring deadline in one place. Bookmark it, and put the dates that apply to you in your own calendar.

::: callout
Deadlines that fall on a weekend or public holiday are usually treated as falling on the next working day, but don't rely on it. File early.
:::

## Every month

| Deadline | What's due | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Within 7 days of paying salaries** | Pension contributions to each employee's pension fund administrator | Employers covered by the pension scheme |
| **10th** | **PAYE**: tax deducted from staff salaries in the previous month, to the state tax authority | Every employer |
| **21st** | **VAT return** and payment for the previous month: nil returns included | Businesses above the ₦100m VAT threshold. See [VAT for small businesses](/blog/vat-registration-small-business-nigeria/) |
| **21st** | **Withholding tax** deducted in the previous month | Businesses that deduct WHT from qualifying payments |

## Every year

| Deadline | What's due | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **31 January** | Employer's annual PAYE return for the previous year | Every employer |
| **31 March** | **Personal income tax return** for the previous year | Every individual with income, including business name owners and partners |
| **30 May** | **NDPC Compliance Audit Return**: NDPC's current deadline | Businesses in the data-protection tiers required to file. See [NDPA compliance](/blog/ndpa-compliance-nigerian-business/) |
| **30 June** | **CAC annual returns for business names** | Every business name, except in the year it was registered |
| **30 June** | **Company income tax return** for companies with a 31 December year end (six months after year end) | Every company: small companies included, at 0%. See [no profit, still file?](/blog/no-profit-still-file-tax-returns-nigeria/) |
| **30 June – 31 December** | **CAC annual returns for incorporated trustees**, with the year's accounts | Every incorporated trustee, except in the year it was incorporated |
| **42 days after your AGM** | **CAC annual returns for companies** | Every company. See below for how to work out your date |

## Working out your company's CAC annual return date

A company's CAC annual return date depends on its **incorporation date** and its **AGM**, which in turn usually follows its **financial year end**:

1. **No return in the year of incorporation.**
2. **The first AGM** must be held within **18 months** of incorporation.
3. **The annual return** is due **within 42 days after the AGM**.
4. After that, **once every year**, 42 days after each year's AGM.

::: stat
42 days
After the AGM: the deadline for a company's CAC annual return.
:::

### Worked examples

| Company | Key date | Annual return due by |
|---|---|---|
| Established company, 31 December year end, AGM held on 30 June | AGM 30 June | **11 August** |
| Incorporated 15 March 2025, first AGM held on the last allowed day | First AGM by 15 September 2026 (18 months) | **27 October 2026** |
| Same company, AGM held on 30 June each year after that | AGM 30 June 2027 | **11 August 2027** |

Small companies and single-member companies may not need to hold an AGM, but they **still file an annual return every year**. The CAC portal shows each company's due date. If you're not sure of yours, send us your RC number and we'll work it out.

Late CAC filings attract penalties, and companies that stop filing can be struck off. See [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/) and [reviving an inactive company](/blog/revive-inactive-company-unfiled-annual-returns/).

## Deadlines on your own dates

| Item | When |
|---|---|
| Company income tax, if your year end isn't 31 December | Six months after your financial year end |
| Trademark renewal | Before the registration expires: 7 years from filing, then every 14 years. See [trademark timelines](/blog/trademark-registration-timeline-renewal-nigeria/) |
| CERPAC and expatriate quota renewals | Before they expire. See [CERPAC renewal](/blog/cerpac-renewal-nigeria/) |
| Licences and permits | On each licence's renewal date. See [which licences you need](/blog/licences-new-business-nigeria/) |
| Tax clearance certificate | Yearly, when you need a current one. See [tax clearance certificates](/blog/tax-clearance-certificate-nigeria/) |

## A sample year for a small company (31 December year end)

| Month | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Every month** | PAYE by the 10th; WHT (and VAT, if above ₦100m) by the 21st; pension within 7 days of payroll |
| **January** | Employer's annual PAYE return by 31 January |
| **March** | Owners' and directors' personal tax returns by 31 March |
| **May** | NDPC audit return by 30 May, if your business must file |
| **June** | Company income tax return by 30 June; hold the AGM |
| **August** | CAC annual return within 42 days of the AGM |

## The short version

Monthly: pension within 7 days of payroll, PAYE by the 10th, VAT and WHT by the 21st. Yearly: employer PAYE return 31 January, personal returns 31 March, NDPC audit return 30 May, business name annual returns and December-year-end company tax returns 30 June, incorporated trustees' returns between 30 June and 31 December, and company annual returns 42 days after the AGM.

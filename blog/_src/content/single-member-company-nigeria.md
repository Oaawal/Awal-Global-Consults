**Yes.** Since the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA 2020), a private company can be formed with **just one shareholder**, and a **small company** can have **just one director**. Before 2020, every company needed at least two members — which is why so many founders added a relative "just to make up the numbers".

## What CAMA 2020 allows

| Requirement | Small private company | Other private companies |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum shareholders | **One** | One |
| Minimum directors | **One** | Two |
| Company secretary | **Not required** | Required |
| Minimum share capital | ₦100,000 | ₦100,000 |

A **small company** is a private company that meets the conditions in CAMA on turnover, assets and ownership — for example, it can't have foreign shareholders or be owned by the government. Most new, locally owned companies qualify at the start.

::: callout
If the company grows beyond the small-company limits, it must meet the requirements for other companies — including **appointing a second director** and a **company secretary**.
:::

## Why choose a one-person company over a business name?

| | Business name | One-person company |
|---|---|---|
| Legal identity | You and the business are the same | The company is separate from you |
| Your liability | Unlimited | Limited to your shares |
| Bringing in partners or investors later | Requires restructuring | Issue or transfer shares |
| Banks, big clients, exporting | Often limited | Generally accepted |
| Cost and admin | Lower | Higher — annual returns, records, tax filings |

If you're starting small and testing an idea, a business name can be enough — see [business name vs limited company](/blog/business-name-vs-limited-company-nigeria/). If you want limited liability, credibility with banks and room to grow, a one-person company is now the straightforward route.

## Things to plan for as a sole owner

- **Who acts if you can't?** With one director, illness or travel can stall decisions and bank transactions. Consider a trusted alternate signatory or a second director.
- **Succession.** Your shares pass under your will or the law on your death. Think about who should own and run the company.
- **Keep company and personal money separate.** Mixing them weakens the protection a company gives you.
- **Annual returns and tax filings** still apply every year — see [CAC annual returns](/blog/cac-annual-returns-deadline-penalty/).

## The short version

One person can now own a Nigerian company and, if it's a small company, be its only director with no company secretary. Plan for who acts when you can't, keep finances separate, and add directors as you grow.

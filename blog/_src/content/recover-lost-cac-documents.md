Banks, tax offices, tenders and clients all ask for your CAC documents. If the certificate, status report or company documents are lost, there are two ways to get them back, depending on whether you can still reach the account the registration was filed under.

## Route 1: download them again

If the registration was filed online and you can still log in to the account used for it:

1. Log in to CAC's registration portal (icrp.cac.gov.ng) with that account.
2. Open the application and check that its status is **Approved**.
3. Click **Action**, then **Download Certificate and Status Report**.

This is the quickest route, and it is the same download you had when the registration was first approved.

::: callout
If a consultant or agent filed for you, the application sits in their account. Ask them for copies first. If you cannot reach them, use Route 2.
:::

## Route 2: apply for certified true copies (CTC)

If you cannot get into the account, the registration was done years ago, or you need the full company file rather than the certificate, apply to CAC for **certified true copies** of the documents.

1. Log in to CAC's portal (icrp.cac.gov.ng) and go to the **Post-Incorporation** portal.
2. Choose the request for certified true copies and find the business name, company or incorporated trustees by its name or BN, RC or IT number.
3. Select the documents you need, such as the certificate, status report, memorandum and articles, or particulars of directors and shareholders.
4. Pay the CTC fees online. **Fees apply to every CTC request** and depend on the documents you ask for.
5. Download or collect the certified copies once CAC processes the request.

A CTC is CAC's own certification that the copy matches its records, so it is often accepted where a photocopy is not. See [certified true copies of CAC documents](/blog/cac-certified-true-copy/) for when banks, courts and embassies ask for them.

## You can request documents for any company

CTC requests are not limited to your own business. You can apply for certified copies of the filed documents of **any** registered business name, company or incorporated trustees. This is useful for due diligence before a deal, a contract or a court matter. See [verifying a company](/blog/verify-company-cac-portal/).

## Before you apply

- **Check the record is current.** A CTC certifies what is on file. If your directors, shareholders or address changed and were not filed, the copies will show the old details. See [changes after CAC registration](/blog/change-business-name-address-partners-cac/).
- **Ask which documents are needed.** A bank may only want the certificate and status report, while a court or embassy may want the full set.
- **Keep copies safe once you have them.** Save PDFs in more than one place.

## The short version

If you can still log in to the account the registration was filed under, download the certificate and status report again from the Action menu. If you cannot, apply for certified true copies through the Post-Incorporation portal on CAC's ICRP. CTCs carry a fee, and you can request them for any registered company, not only your own.

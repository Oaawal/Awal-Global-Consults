Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we get asked most often.

How long does CAC business registration take?

Business name registration typically takes 3–7 working days after we receive all required documents and payment. Limited company (RC) registration takes 7–14 working days. We'll keep you updated throughout the process.

What documents do I need to register my business?

For business name registration, you'll need a valid government-issued ID (NIN slip, National ID, or International Passport), a proposed business name, your address, and nature of business. We'll guide you through everything once you contact us.

Can I register my business from outside Lagos?

Absolutely. We serve clients across all states in Nigeria. Everything is handled remotely — you send documents via WhatsApp or email, and we take care of the rest. No need to travel or visit any office.

Are your fees all-inclusive? Any hidden charges?

Our pricing is transparent. The fees we quote include government fees, our service charge, and all processing costs. We will always tell you the exact total upfront before you proceed — no surprise charges.

Is my personal and business information safe?

Yes. We treat all client information with strict confidentiality. Your documents and personal details are never shared with third parties. We operate professionally and ethically in handling all client data.

Do I need a lawyer to work with you?

No. You don't need to hire a separate lawyer for the services we provide. Our practice is led by a trained legal professional, and we handle everything on your behalf from start to finish.

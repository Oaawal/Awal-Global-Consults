Frequently asked questions

More answers in our guides.

How long does CAC business registration take?

Business name registration is completed within 24 hours of receiving your documents and payment. Limited company (RC) registration usually takes 48–72 hours if CAC raises no queries. Incorporated Trustees registration usually takes about 5 to 6 weeks: name approval (about 3 business days), a 28-day newspaper publication window during which the trustees sign their declarations, then final approval (about 3 business days). We keep you updated throughout.

What documents do I need to register my business?

For business name registration, you'll need a valid government-issued ID (NIN slip, National ID, or International Passport), a proposed business name, your address, and nature of business. We'll guide you through everything once you contact us.

Can I register my business from outside Lagos?

Absolutely. We serve clients across all states in Nigeria. Everything is handled remotely: you send documents via WhatsApp or email, and we take care of the rest. No need to travel or visit any office.

Are your fees all-inclusive? Any hidden charges?

We work on a custom quote basis. Once we understand exactly what you need, we send an itemized quote that includes government fees, our service charge, and all processing costs: the full total upfront before you proceed, with no surprise charges.

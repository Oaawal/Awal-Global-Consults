Who has to register with the NDPC?

Data controllers and processors of major importance: organisations that process the personal data of more than 200 people in any six months, provide commercial ICT services on other people’s devices, or operate in a designated sector such as education, health, financial services, hospitality or e-commerce.

How much is NDPC registration?

The NDPC fee is ₦250,000 for Ultra-High Level, ₦100,000 for Extra-High Level and ₦10,000 for Ordinary-High Level organisations, renewed yearly, plus a 50% late penalty now that the registration period has ended. Ultra-High Level organisations also pay ₦5,000 a year for each data processor they engage.

Who files the annual compliance audit return?

Ultra-High and Extra-High Level organisations, every year by 31 March, with a verification statement from a licensed DPCO.

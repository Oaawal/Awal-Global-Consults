Where Founders Get Stuck

The walls fintech startups hit

These problems usually surface after months of building, often in a single conversation with a bank or partner. They are much cheaper to find early.

The licensing warranty

A banking-as-a-service agreement asks you to confirm you hold, or don't need, a CBN licence for what you do. Signing it without checking can make you liable for the partner's regulatory risk.

Objects that don't fit

Your company was registered for "general merchandise" or "ICT services", but you're running payments, wallets or lending. Banks and regulators check the MEMART.

Building in the wrong category

Product decisions quietly put you into a licensed activity, such as holding customer funds, without a licence or a partner arrangement that covers it.

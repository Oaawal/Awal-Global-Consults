Good to know

What is a trademark class?

Nigeria uses the Nice Classification: 45 classes covering every type of goods (classes 1 to 34) and services (classes 35 to 45). Your trademark protects you only in the classes you register.

Can I register a trademark in more than one class?

Yes, but in Nigeria each class is a separate application with its own fee, so register in the classes your business actually uses.

How long does a Nigerian trademark last?

A registration lasts 7 years before it needs renewing.

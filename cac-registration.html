What is CAC Registration?

Giving your business a legal identity

CAC registration is the process of officially registering your business with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in Nigeria.

It gives your business a legal identity, allowing you to operate properly, open business bank accounts, build credibility, and access opportunities that require a registered entity.

Without proper registration, many businesses struggle with trust, limitations, and long-term growth.

Why it matters

Why registering your business is important

Credibility

A registered business is taken more seriously by clients, partners, and institutions.

Access

You can open a business bank account and access more opportunities.

Protection

Your business name is protected and properly recognized.

Governance · Risk · Compliance

Run your business properly, protect it, and stay on the right side of the rules

Governance, risk and compliance (GRC) is what keeps a business trustworthy to banks, clients, investors and regulators. For a small or growing Nigerian business, that means a properly run company, protection against fraud and cyber risk, and every registration, filing and licence in order. We bring all three together, in plain language.

See the GRC Review Book a Free 20-Minute Call

Three pillars:

  • Governance: a company that is properly run and documented
  • Risk: protection against fraud, cyber threats and bad contracts
  • Compliance: every registration, filing and licence in order

The GRC Review

One review, one report, one fix-it list

The fastest way to know where your business stands. We check all three pillars and give you a clear, prioritised plan. Fixed, itemised quote before any work starts.

Governance check

CAC status and filing history, directors, shareholders and persons with significant control, MEMART objects against what you actually do, and the resolutions and records you should have.

Risk check

Account security, payment procedures, staff awareness, backups, customer data handling, and the contracts and partners your business depends on.

Compliance check

TIN and tax filings, VAT position, SCUML, data protection, sector licences and the deadlines coming up in the next twelve months.

You get: a written GRC report with a one-page summary, findings for each pillar, and a fix-it list ranked do this week, do this month and plan for later. Where something needs filing or fixing, we can do it for you on an itemised quote.
